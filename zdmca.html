DMCA / Copyright Policy

Last updated: August 2026

Parchrome respects the intellectual property rights of others and expects anything we link to or reference to do the same. If you believe content on Parchrome, or content on a third-party site we link to, infringes your copyright, we want to hear from you.

1. What we can act on

Parchrome doesn't host game files, videos, or media ourselves — the site is a directory and guide resource that links out to other websites. If a link we list points to infringing content, let us know and we'll review and remove the listing where appropriate. We can't remove content hosted on a third-party site directly; that request should also go to the site actually hosting the material.

2. How to submit a takedown request

To report a claim of copyright infringement, please send us the following information through our Discord or via the site's feedback form:

  • A description of the copyrighted work you believe has been infringed;
  • The specific Parchrome page and/or link you're reporting;
  • Your contact information (email or a way to reach you);
  • A statement that you have a good-faith belief the use is unauthorized;
  • A statement, under penalty of perjury, that the information provided is accurate and that you're authorized to act on behalf of the copyright owner.

3. What happens next

We'll review valid requests and remove or update the reported listing where we're able to. We may reach out to you for clarification if a request is incomplete.

4. Repeat infringers

If we determine that a listed resource is consistently and primarily used for copyright infringement, we'll remove it from the site rather than evaluate it link-by-link.

5. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as the site evolves. Continued use of Parchrome after updates means you accept the revised policy.
