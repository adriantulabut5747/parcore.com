1. In-browser vocal isolation

Runs instantly, no server or API. Uses center-channel cancellation — subtracts the right channel from the left, which removes audio that's panned dead-center in the stereo mix (often vocals). It's a rough trick, not real AI separation, but it works for a quick proof of concept.

2. Real 4-stem separation (Demucs)

There is no API key for this. Demucs is a model that runs on your own computer — you separate a song from the terminal, and it writes four WAV files. Load all four here and they play locked in sync, so muting one part leaves the rest of the band playing.

Center-channel cancellation only works on stereo tracks where vocals are mixed dead-center — it won't cleanly isolate drums or a specific guitar, and it'll sound thin/phasey. For anything close to production quality (splitting drums, bass, lead guitar separately), you need a real model like Demucs — Panel 2 is that real model — Demucs, running locally, no service and no key.
