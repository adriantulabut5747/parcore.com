A

Badges on the art

Closest to today, but cleaner: source and tag move onto the picture as frosted chips, so the footer row disappears. Hover: card lifts, picture zooms, a red line wipes across its bottom edge, title turns red.

B

Poster

The picture is the whole card, text sits on a dark fade at the bottom. Source is an icon-only key in the corner. Hover: the description rises out from under the title and the picture zooms and warms up. Strongest "wow", least text visible at rest.

C

Framed well

Our raised-UI recipe all the way through: the picture sits sunken in the box, the pack's icon is a raised key overlapping its edge, the source is a black corner tab (like the old rank badge), tags are little wells. Hover: the icon hops up and tilts, the picture brightens.

D

Tilt and glare

The card leans toward your cursor in 3D with a soft light following it, and the picture drifts the other way (parallax). The source is a grey key hanging off the picture. Needs a few lines of JS; touch screens and reduced-motion get a flat card.

E

Pixel corners

The Minecraft one: stepped pixel corners, the name and author on a frosted bar over the picture, and an XP bar along the bottom whose ten segments fill red, one after another, on hover. Would only suit the Minecraft pages; CoC would get its own flavour.
