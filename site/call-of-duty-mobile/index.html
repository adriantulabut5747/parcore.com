Weapon Classes

What each class is for, so you know which builds to look at.

Assault RifleMid range

The all-rounder. Good damage and control at most distances. The safest first class to learn.

 SMGClose range

Fastest movement and aim-down-sights speed. Built for rushers who take fights around corners.

 Sniper RifleLong range

Kills in one shot to the upper body, but slow to aim. Best for holding long lines of sight.

 ShotgunVery close

One shot up close, nearly useless past a few metres. Strongest on small maps.

 LMGMid to long

Huge magazine and steady fire, but heavy. Holds a lane or an objective for a long time.

 Marksman RifleMid to long

Semi-auto or bolt-action rifles between an assault rifle and a sniper. High damage per shot, less forgiving.

Scorestreaks and Operator Skills

The two kinds of power-up you bring into a Multiplayer match.

Scorestreaks

You equip three. Each unlocks at a set score earned in one life, and dying resets your progress. Cheap streaks get used far more often than big ones, so carry one you'll earn every match.

  1. EarlyUAV, Counter-UAV
  2. MiddleHunter Killer Drone, Predator Missile, Sentry Gun
  3. LateVTOL Escort, Stealth Chopper, Chopper Gunner

Operator Skills

One per loadout. It charges during the match from kills, objective play and time, then gives you a short, strong ability.

  • PurifierFlamethrower
  • Death MachineMinigun
  • War MachineGrenade launcher
  • SparrowExplosive bow
  • Gravity SpikesArea kill on landing
  • Ballistic ShieldBlocks bullets

Game Modes

The goal of each mode in one line, and one tip to win it.

Team Deathmatch

First team to the kill limit wins.

Trade kills with a teammate. Never take a 1v2.

Domination Ranked

Hold flags A, B and C to earn points over time.

Hold two flags, not three. Chasing the third flips your spawns.

Hardpoint Ranked

One zone moves around the map. Stand in it to score.

Head to the next zone before this one ends.

Search & Destroy Ranked

One life per round. Attackers plant the bomb, defenders stop them.

Information wins rounds. Don't peek first when you're the last one alive.

Kill Confirmed

Kills only count when someone picks up the dog tag.

Deny enemy tags near your teammates' bodies.

Free-for-All

Everyone for themselves. First to the kill limit wins.

Fight at the edges of the map, not the middle.

Gun Game

Every kill swaps you to the next weapon. Finish the list first to win.

A melee kill sets the victim back one weapon.

Battle Royale Ranked

Drop in, loot, and be the last squad alive while the zone shrinks.

Rotate early. Fighting your way in at the zone edge costs fights.

How Ranked Works

Seven tiers, the same ladder in Multiplayer and Battle Royale.

  1. 01Rookie
  2. 02Veteran
  3. 03Elite
  4. 04Pro
  5. 05Master
  6. 06Grandmaster
  7. 07Legendary

Two separate ranks

Multiplayer and Battle Royale each have their own rank. Climbing one does nothing for the other.

How you climb

Multiplayer ranked rotates objective modes such as Hardpoint and Search & Destroy, and winning matters most. In Battle Royale, placement and kills both earn points.

Season resets

Ranked seasons run on their own schedule, not the battle pass. When one ends you get rewards for the highest tier you reached, and your rank drops back a few tiers.

First-Time Setup

Settings to change before your first ranked match.

  • Shooting modeSimple mode fires on its own when an enemy is under your crosshair. Advanced lets you choose when to shoot.
    Advanced
  • Frame rateA steadier, higher frame rate helps your aim more than better graphics do.
    Highest available
  • Graphics qualityLower it until the highest frame rate option unlocks and stays smooth.
    As low as needed
  • SensitivityMultiplayer and Battle Royale have separate sensitivity settings. Change them in small steps and play a few matches before changing them again.
    Small steps
  • GyroscopeTilting the phone to fine-tune your aim. Many players use it only while aiming down sights.
    Optional
  • Custom layoutMove fire, aim, crouch and jump to where your fingers already rest, so you can shoot while moving.
    Make it yours

Glossary

Words you'll hear in lobbies and videos, in plain English.

ADS
Aim down sights: looking through your gun's scope or sight.
Hipfire
Shooting without aiming down sights. Faster to start, less accurate.
TTK
Time to kill: how fast a gun kills someone at full health.
Quickscope
Aiming a sniper and shooting almost instantly.
Drop shot
Going prone while shooting so your head drops out of the enemy's aim.
Slide cancel
Stopping a slide early to aim and shoot sooner.
Pre-aim
Keeping your crosshair where an enemy will appear before they do.
Jiggle peek
Stepping in and out of cover quickly to see without getting shot.
Flank
Going around the side to hit enemies from behind.
Spawn trap
Holding the area where the enemy team keeps coming back in.
Wallbang
Shooting an enemy through a thin wall or door.
Rotate
In Battle Royale: moving to the next safe zone.
Third party
In Battle Royale: attacking two squads while they fight each other.

Frequently Asked Questions

Where do these builds come from?

Each build on our attachments page was copied from our own Gunsmith screens in game. The ones with a build code were exported straight from the game.

How do I use a build code?

Tap Copy on a build, then paste the code into the Gunsmith's build code import in game. The game fills in the attachments for you.

Should I use different builds for Multiplayer and Battle Royale?

Usually, yes. Battle Royale fights happen at longer range, so BR builds favour range and recoil control. Multiplayer builds favour moving fast and aiming down sights quickly. Each build is tagged MP, BR or both.

When does the current season end?

The battle pass countdown near the top estimates it from the length of recent seasons. Activision usually confirms the exact date a few days before a new season starts.

Is Parchrome an official Call of Duty site?

No. Parchrome is a fan site. It isn't affiliated with Activision or TiMi Studio Group.
