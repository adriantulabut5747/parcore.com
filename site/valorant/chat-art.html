Put it in chat

JettCopy, open chat, paste, send. Do it in the buy phase, not while I'm entering site.

  1. Press Copy art under the maker, or Copy on any gallery card.
  2. In a match, press Enter to open team chat, or Shift + Enter for all chat.
  3. Paste with Ctrl + V and send. Chat breaks the line into rows, and the rows stack into your drawing.
