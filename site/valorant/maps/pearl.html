How to play Pearl

JettAttack and defend tips for every site, plus the tricks Pearl hides. Read fast, I'm already on site!

Riot's first map with no mechanics at all. Long lanes favour rifles and the Operator, and mid control decides the round.

Map tricks

  • No doors, ropes or teleporters.
  • Long sightlines everywhere: rifles and the Operator do well here.

ASite

Attack

  • Clear Secret and A Link as you come in: that's where A defenders hide.

Defend

  • Hold A from Secret or A Link.

BSite

Attack

  • Check B Tower before you commit to the site.

Defend

  • Hold B Tower against a B Main push: attackers clear the low angles first.

Mid

Attack

  • Win mid and you can hit either site, or cut behind the defenders.

Defend

  • A trap or turret near your spawn warns you of a flank.
