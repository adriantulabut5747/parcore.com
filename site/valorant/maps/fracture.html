How to play Fracture

JettAttack and defend tips for every site, plus the tricks Fracture hides. Read fast, I'm already on site!

An H-shaped map: the sites are on opposite sides, defenders spawn between them, and attackers use ziplines to reach the far side and pinch.

Map tricks

  • Attackers spawn in one place but reach the far side on two one-way ziplines that pass under defender spawn.
  • During the buy phase, defenders can't hear attackers on the ziplines.
  • On a zipline you can't stop, turn back, use abilities or let go.
  • If the spike carrier dies on a zipline, the spike lands on the nearer side.
  • A Hall's door opens by itself, loudly, when anyone gets close, from either side.
  • Four ult orbs, one in each quarter of the map.

ASite

Attack

  • Hit A from both sides at once: A Main, and A Dish across the zipline.

Defend

  • A Hall's door opens loudly when someone nears it. That's a free warning.

BSite

Attack

  • Pinch B from B Main and B Arcade together.

Defend

  • Hold from B Tower, and use smokes or walls to slow a fast rotation through the site.

Rotations

Attack

  • Use the ziplines to fake one site and arrive at the other.

Defend

  • You spawn between the sites, so your rotations are short: give up space and retake together.
