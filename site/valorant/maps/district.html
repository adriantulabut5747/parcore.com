How Team Deathmatch works

JettThe mode in one breath. Then go get some kills, I'll be right behind you. Okay, in front.

Team Deathmatch is 5v5: first team to 100 kills, or the most kills after 9.5 minutes. The match runs in four stages that unlock better guns, weapon spawners hand out more, and you respawn 1.5 seconds after dying.
