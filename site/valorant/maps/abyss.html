How to play Abyss

JettAttack and defend tips for every site, plus the tricks Abyss hides. Read fast, I'm already on site!

The first map with no outer walls. Edges, a drop in mid and narrow ledges change how every fight near the rim plays out.

Map tricks

  • No outer walls, and a drop in mid: fall off and you die.
  • Danger (B) and Secret (A) are narrow ledges on the rim, used as jump shortcuts.
  • Catwalk is reached only by jumping the mid drop to an ascender, from Top or Library.
  • Ropes: A Site up to A Tower, B Main up to Mid Bend, and one in attacker spawn.
  • Both Vent entrances have doors with 120 HP. Break one and it stays open for the round.

ASite

Attack

  • Break a Vent door before you need it: it's a second way in that stays open all round.
  • Secret's ledge reaches A from an angle defenders forget, but one bad jump is a death.

Defend

  • A Tower looks down over A Site, and the rope from the site takes you straight up.
  • A Vent door breaking is a warning: someone is coming through Vent.

BSite

Attack

  • Danger's ledge is a shortcut onto B, but miss the jump and you're dead.

Defend

  • Fight away from the edges: a knockback or a bad step there ends your round.

Mid

Attack

  • The rope from B Main up to Mid Bend links B to mid: use it to join a mid push.

Defend

  • Catwalk is reached from both Top and Library: watch both ways up.
