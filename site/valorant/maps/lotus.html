How to play Lotus

JettAttack and defend tips for every site, plus the tricks Lotus hides. Read fast, I'm already on site!

Three sites, so defenders are spread thin. Two rotating doors and a breakable wall decide how fast anyone gets from one site to another.

Map tricks

  • Two rotating doors: one between A Main and A Tree, one between C Mound and B Main. A button on either side spins them.
  • A turning door is loud and shows on the map for everyone.
  • The wall between A Main and A Link has 400 HP. Break it to open a new path for the round.
  • A rope at the back of A Site goes up to A Top, and walking off A Top down to A Drop makes no sound.

ASite

Attack

  • Split A from A Main and A Tree at once, so defenders can't hold both.
  • Smoke A Top, Drop and Stairs: that's where the A defenders play.

Defend

  • Choke A Main and A Tree, and leave a trap or turret for flanks.
  • Walk, don't run, off A Top: land close to the edge and nobody hears it.

BSite

Attack

  • B is all angles: go in only with strong smokes and clear calls.

Defend

  • Smoke B Main to stall the push, then retake with your team.

CSite

Attack

  • Flash C Main or Mound before you peek: an Operator often waits there.
  • Plant near Waterfall or Hall so you can guard the spike without peeking.

Defend

  • Make C Main your choke point.

Retakes

Attack

  • A door that stays silent tells you nobody is rotating through it.

Defend

  • Don't stack all five on a retake: send one player around the back.
