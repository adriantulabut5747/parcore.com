How to play Bind

JettAttack and defend tips for every site, plus the tricks Bind hides. Read fast, I'm already on site!

Bind has no mid. Two one-way teleporters link the sites instead, and every teleport is heard across the map.

Map tricks

  • Two one-way teleporters: A Short to B Short, and B Long to A Lobby.
  • Every teleport makes a sound the whole map hears.
  • The exit door opens only from inside, but bullets go through it.
  • Abilities, the spike and dropped guns can go through a teleporter too.

ASite

Attack

  • Hit A from Bath and A Short together.
  • Teleport from B Long to fake B, then arrive in A Lobby.

Defend

  • Hear a teleport? Someone is on the move: call it and adjust.

BSite

Attack

  • Enter B from Long and Window at once, so defenders can't hold both.

Defend

  • Slow the jump down from B Window with a slow or a wall.
