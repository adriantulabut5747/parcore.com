How to play Sunset

JettAttack and defend tips for every site, plus the tricks Sunset hides. Read fast, I'm already on site!

A traditional two-site, three-lane map. Its one gadget is the door between B Market and Mid Courtyard.

Map tricks

  • The door between B Market and Mid Courtyard opens and closes with a switch on the Market side.
  • Closed, the door stops bullets, but it has 500 HP. Break it and it stays open for the round.
  • With the door gone, there's a long sightline from B Site into mid.

ASite

Attack

  • A is tight corridors and close fights: flash the near corners before you swing.

BSite

Attack

  • Break the Market door from mid to open a second way onto B.

Defend

  • Close the Market door to cut mid's route into B: attackers need 500 damage to get through.

Mid

Attack

  • Mid is a horseshoe with lots of ways in: take it as a group with utility, not alone.

Defend

  • Watch Courtyard: it's mid's shortcut into B Market through the door.
