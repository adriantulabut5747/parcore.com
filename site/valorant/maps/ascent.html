How to play Ascent

JettAttack and defend tips for every site, plus the tricks Ascent hides. Read fast, I'm already on site!

The open mid decides the round, and each site's door can shut off one way in, for either team.

Map tricks

  • Each site has a door (A Link on A, Market on B) that starts open. Its switch is on the site side: next to the door on A, on a table in Toolshed on B.
  • Closed, a door stops bullets, but it has 500 HP. Break it and it stays open for the round.
  • The two walls between Courtyard and Link have 400 HP panels. Break one and you can shoot through it for the round.
  • A Window's glass breaks on the first bullet or thrown ability.

ASite

Attack

  • After you plant, close the A Link door: the retake from mid has one way less.

Defend

  • Close the A Link door to stop a push from mid into A.

BSite

Attack

  • Plant, then close the Market door from Toolshed so the retake can't come through Market.

Defend

  • Boat House gives a cross angle on B Main.

Mid

Attack

  • Smoke Catwalk and Top, then send recon: mid control opens both sites.

Defend

  • Break the Courtyard panels early if you want to spray through the walls into Link.
