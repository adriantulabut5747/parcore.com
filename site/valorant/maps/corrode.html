How to play Corrode

JettAttack and defend tips for every site, plus the tricks Corrode hides. Read fast, I'm already on site!

Corrode is a classic two-site, three-lane map. Mid is narrow and packed with cover, and it's the lane for lurks and quick rotations.

Map tricks

  • No doors, ropes or teleporters: Corrode is the second standard map with no mechanics (Pearl was the first).
  • Both sites sit low, so defenders often get to shoot down at you from above.

All sites

Attack

  • Pressure mid first, then split into a site from two sides instead of all five through one choke.
  • On a B hit, smoke B Tower and B Link before you walk in.

Defend

  • Call contact and how many you see before anyone rotates.
  • Watch mid for lurkers: it's the short way to a flank.

Mid

Attack

  • Mid control is the key fight here: win it and you can hit either site.
