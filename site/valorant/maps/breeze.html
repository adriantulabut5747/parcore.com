How to play Breeze

JettAttack and defend tips for every site, plus the tricks Breeze hides. Read fast, I'm already on site!

Breeze is wide open, so long-range fights decide it. The rope on A and the automatic door in mid are its only gadgets.

Map tricks

  • The largest and most open map: bring rifles and the Operator.
  • A rope at the back of A Site lifts defenders up to Bridge and Hall.
  • Mid Hall's door opens by itself, loudly, from either side, and for anything thrown at it.

ASite

Attack

  • Push A from two sides: part of the team on the main path, the rest through Mid Hall.

Defend

  • Keep someone near Hall so A doesn't get flanked.
  • The rope up to Bridge is your fast rotation off A.

BSite

Attack

  • Hit B from B Main and Mid Top together, so the defenders have to split.

Defend

  • One player on site, one in Mid Nest watching mid.

Mid

Attack

  • When nobody holds Mid Nest, push through Wood Doors.

Defend

  • Always keep someone in Mid Nest to call mid pushes.
