How to play Icebox

JettAttack and defend tips for every site, plus the tricks Icebox hides. Read fast, I'm already on site!

Icebox is all about height. Both sites have raised spots, and ropes and ziplines get you up to them.

Map tricks

  • The A zipline was the first horizontal zipline in the game.
  • Both sites have raised spots you climb by rope, and the spike can be planted up there.
  • High ground matters here: most fights are about height.

ASite

Attack

  • Clear the high spots first: Rafters, Nest and Pipes, before you plant.

Defend

  • Play the high ground: it's the strongest angle on A.

BSite

Attack

  • Recon B before you walk in: the containers hide a lot of corners.

Defend

  • Tube is a sneaky way to get a second angle on the B push.

Mid

Attack

  • Taking mid needs the player there alive: if they die early, the team is split.

Defend

  • One player holds mid, and Kitchen is the fast way to back them up.
