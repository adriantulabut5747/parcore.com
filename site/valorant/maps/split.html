How to play Split

JettAttack and defend tips for every site, plus the tricks Split hides. Read fast, I'm already on site!

Split is vertical: a raised mid and a tower on each site. It was the first map with ropes.

Map tricks

  • Ropes: two from Sewer up to A Lobby, one from B Hell up to B Tower, and four that run through Vent.
  • Each site has a tower looming over it.

ASite

Attack

  • Expect defenders up high: clear Rafters, Screens and the site boxes, and scout before you walk on.

Defend

  • Slow the A push with a wall, a trap or a turret, then fight what gets through.

BSite

Attack

  • Clear B Back, Stairs and Garage before you plant: that's where B defenders hide.

Defend

  • Delay B with smokes and retake as a group.

Mid

Attack

  • Clear Vent, Mail and the ropes: that's where mid defenders sit.

Defend

  • Listen for audio cues before you rotate.
