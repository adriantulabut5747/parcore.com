How RR works

JettWins give you RR, losses take it away, and 100 RR moves you up a division. Win big for a bigger jump. Me? I just win.

Win or lose

The result sets the base amount. A loss never gives you RR, however well you played.

Round difference

A 13-2 win gives more RR than a 13-9 win.

Your play

Counts for less than the result, and only from Iron to Ascendant. From Immortal up, only wins and losses count.

Hidden MMR

If your hidden skill rating is above your rank, you gain more and lose less until your rank catches up. Below it, the opposite.

Moving up
Reach 100 RR to go up a division. You start the next one with at least 10 RR, and a big win can skip a division (Gold 1 to Gold 3).
Dropping down
Hit 0 RR, then lose again. You land on at least 70 RR in the division below.
Rank shields
Reaching division 1 of a rank gives you 2 shields. Each one blocks a drop into the rank below. They don't stop drops between divisions of the same rank, and dodges or remakes don't use one.
Immortal and up
Immortal 1 starts at 10 RR. Immortal 2, Immortal 3 and Radiant go by RR cutoffs set for each region (below), and Radiant also needs a top 500 spot.

Solo, duo, trio or a 5-stack. Four-player parties can't queue. Immortal 1 and up can only go solo, duo or in a 5-stack.
5-stacks
Any ranks can play together, but for less RR. Everyone Ascendant 3 or below: 25% less, and only if someone is outside the limits above. Anyone Immortal: 25% less. Anyone Radiant: 75% less, or 90% if someone in the party is below Radiant.

Placements and resets

JettYou need account level 20 to play ranked, then a few placement matches set your rank. Don't dodge them, I'm watching.

  1. Competitive unlocks at account level 20.
  2. At the start of a season and at mid-season, everyone plays 5 placement matches. You start on 50 RR in the rank you land.
  3. Nobody places straight into Immortal: the highest first rank is Ascendant.
  4. At every other act, 1 placement match updates your rank, and a bad one can drop it. Immortal and Radiant players lose 90% of their RR there, and Radiants go back to Immortal.
  5. Ranks never decay, but Immortal and Radiant players must play at least once every 7 days to stay on the leaderboard.
  6. Lost RR to a cheater? Riot refunds some of it, up to a limit each act.
