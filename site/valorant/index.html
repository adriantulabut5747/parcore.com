    Economy Cheat Sheet

    Verified vs current patch

    Every credit decision Valorant forces on you, in one place — buy thresholds, round rewards, the loss bonus curve, and full weapon pricing.

    Buy Thresholds

    Save0–1,900

    Buy nothing. Bank it — a lost round with a saved rifle beats a force with a dead one.

    Eco1,900–2,900

    Sidearm upgrade + light shields at most. Play for picks and spike plants, not the round.

    Half Buy / Force2,900–3,900

    SMG or cheap rifle, partial armor. A coordinated team decision, not a panic buy.

    Round Rewards

    Round win+3,000
    Kill (most weapons)+200

    Loss Bonus Curve

    1 1st loss 1,900
    2 2nd loss 2,400
    3+ 3rd+ loss 2,900

    Resets to 1,900 the instant you win a round — winning then losing can leave you worse off than losing twice straight.

    Average Combat Score Breakdown

    Community-verified, unofficial

    Riot has never published the exact ACS formula — but consistent, independently-verified testing across trackers and pro analysts confirms the same weighting every time. Here’s exactly what raises or ignores your score, action by action.

    Value scales with how many enemies were still alive when you got the kill — first blood is worth more than cleaning up the last man standing.

    02Multi-Kill Bonus
    Double kill (2K)+50

    Quadra kill (4K)+150
    Ace (5K)+200

    Stacks on top of kill-order points — a full ace can be worth 750 from kills alone (150+130+110+90+70+200), before damage.

    03Everything Else
    Damage dealtAny source — guns, abilities+1/dmg
    Non-damage assistBlinds, reveals, heals → kill+25
    Spike plantConfirmed: no Combat ScoreNo points
    Spike defuseConfirmed: no Combat ScoreNo points
    Round win/lossScore is per-action, not outcomeNo points

    Why this matters: ACS rewards fragging and damage almost exclusively. Objective plays that actually win rounds — planting, defusing, holding an angle — earn zero Combat Score directly, which is why duelists tend to post higher ACS than sentinels or controllers even when the latter are doing more to win the round. ACS also has no effect on hidden MMR or rank — it only nudges the RR gained or lost within a competitive match, per Riot.

    Rank Rating (RR) Breakdown

    Approximate, changes each patch

    RR is what moves your visible rank. It’s driven by your win/loss, but the size of the swing is shaped by round score, individual performance, and a hidden number you never see: your MMR.

    Base Win/Loss±Round Diff±Performance±MMR Gap=RR Change
    01Base Result
    Typical winStandard competitive game+10 to +25
    Typical lossStandard competitive game-10 to -25
    Round diff bonus13–3 stomp pays more than 13–11Scales with margin

    A blowout win reads as a bigger skill gap than a nail-biter, so the system pays it out accordingly.

    02Performance Bonus
    Iron → AscendantCombat stats can add or subtract RRActive
    Immortal / RadiantWins and round diff onlyNot active

    Standout individual play only tips the scale below Immortal. At the top of the ladder, RR is decided almost entirely by winning and by how hard you win.

    03Hidden MMR Gap
    MMR above rankSystem is “pushing” you upBigger wins, smaller losses
    MMR below rankSystem is “correcting” you downSmaller wins, bigger losses

    MMR is invisible and never shown, but it’s the real driver of your RR swings — two players who go 13–8 in the same game can walk away with very different RR.

    Why the swings feel random: going 29/5 and still losing RR is normal — winning is roughly 90% of the equation, and individual stats are a minor nudge that fades out completely at Immortal+. Riot last recalibrated the RR curve in Patch 13.00 (V26 Act 4, June 2026) to make consistent winners climb more reliably, so exact numbers shift patch to patch — treat the figures above as directional, not literal.
