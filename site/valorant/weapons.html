    How it's worked out

    JettEvery number comes straight from the game files through valorant-api.com, checked once a day. No guessing, promise!

    1. Health is 100. Light shields add 25 and heavy shields add 50, and shields take the damage first, so a kill through heavy shields needs 150.
    2. Shots to kill = that total divided by the gun's damage at that distance and hit zone, rounded up.
    3. Time to kill = the gaps between those shots at the gun's fire rate. The first shot lands at 0 ms. Aiming, recoil and the Odin's spin-up aren't counted, and shotguns get none because their pellets spread.
