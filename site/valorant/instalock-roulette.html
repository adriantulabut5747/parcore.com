    How it works

    JettEvery slice is the same size and the pick is made before the wheel moves, so nobody can rig it. Not even me.

    1. Every agent or gun in your pool gets one slice of the same size. The winner is drawn with your browser's secure random numbers before the wheel starts turning; the spin just shows you where it landed.
    2. Team of 5 spins five times and takes each pick off the wheel, so nobody gets the same agent. With Every role on, the last spins only offer the roles the team is still missing.
    3. Challenge adds one rule to play by, written by us. On the Weapons wheel you never get a challenge that changes your gun.
    4. Copy link saves the whole roll in the address, so a friend opens exactly what you got. Your pool, sound and last 10 spins stay on this device.
