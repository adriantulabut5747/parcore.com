The four roles

JettDuelists take fights, Initiators open them, Controllers block sight lines and Sentinels hold the ground. Riot's own words below. Duelist is the fun one.

Duelists9 agents

Duelists are self-sufficient fraggers who their team expects, through abilities and skills, to get high frags and seek out engagements first.

PhoenixJettRazeReynaYoruNeonIsoWaylayHush

Initiators7 agents

Initiators challenge angles by setting up their team to enter contested ground and push defenders away.

SovaBreachSkyeKAY/OFadeGekkoTejo

Controllers7 agents

Controllers are experts in slicing up dangerous territory to set their team up for success.

BrimstoneViperOmenAstraHarborCloveMiks

Sentinels7 agents

Sentinels are defensive experts who can lock down areas and watch flanks, both on attacker and defender rounds.

CypherSageKilljoyChamberDeadlockVyseVeto

How to unlock agents

JettFive agents are free from day one, the rest cost VP, Kingdom Credits or a token. I'm one of the free five. You're welcome.

Free from your first match

BrimstoneControllerJettDuelistPhoenixDuelistSageSentinelSovaInitiator
  1. Every other agent costs 1,000 VP, 8,000 Kingdom Credits or 1 Free Agent Token in the Agent Store, and stays yours for good.
  2. The Basic Training event gives up to 2 Free Agent Tokens to new players.
  3. A new agent has a 28-day recruitment event from launch: earn 200,000 XP in that time and they unlock for free. Kingdom Credits and tokens can't buy them during it.
  4. Xbox Game Pass unlocks every agent while your subscription lasts.
  5. The Range and All Random One Site let you play any agent, locked or not.
