CBasic ability

Pick-me-up

QBasic ability

Meddle

EQUIP Meddle. FIRE to throw. Explodes on landing, decaying all players.

ESignature ability

Ruse

EQUIP a tactical map. FIRE to set smoke locations. ALT FIRE to cast smokes.

While dead, Clove can access Ruse to cast brief smokes.

XUltimate ability

Not Dead Yet

On death, ACTIVATE to channel a self-resurrect. During the channel, Clove is intangible. REACTIVATE to end the channel early. Once resurrected, secure a kill or damaging assist to stay alive.
