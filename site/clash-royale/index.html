          Golden KnightThe questions players ask me most. Yours isn't here? Drop it in the box and I'll dash back with an answer.

          Where do these decks come from?

          Every morning we read the ranked battles of the Path of Legends top 1,000 and count every deck in them. A deck needs 50 games to be listed, so a lucky streak can't sneak onto the board.

          How do I put a deck into my game?

          On a phone, tap Copy deck and Clash Royale opens with the deck ready to save. On a computer the key copies a link instead: send it to your phone and open it there.

          What does Steal another do?

          It deals a random deck from the 12 most played right now, with its real win rate. Keep tapping until one fits your cards, then copy it or open it in the Deck Builder to make it yours.

          Can I look up my own profile?

          Not yet. Player pages, the full leaderboard and River Race clan pages are next on the list. Until then, Top decks and the Deck Builder are ready to use.
