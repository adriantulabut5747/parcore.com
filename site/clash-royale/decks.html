How it's counted

Golden KnightEvery number here is a real ranked battle from the top 1,000 players, counted fresh every morning. As real as my hair!

  1. Every morning we read the last 30 battles of each player in the Path of Legends top 1,000 and keep only the ranked 1v1 games played since the last count.
  2. Both decks in a battle count. A battle between two top 1,000 players shows up in both their logs but is counted once, and draws are left out.
  3. A deck is its 8 cards, and an evolution or hero counts as part of it: a deck with Evo Knight and the same deck with plain Knight are two decks.
  4. Numbers cover the last 7 days. A deck needs 50 games in that time to be listed, so a lucky 5-0 can't top the board.
  5. Win rate = wins out of games. Usage = the share of all decks played in those battles.
  6. A win rate is only as sure as its game count: with fewer games, the real number could be further either way (hover a win rate to see its range). A green edge on a deck means even the low end of that range is above 50%, a red edge means even the high end is below it, and no colour means it's too close to call.

Ask the Golden Knight

Golden KnightThe questions players ask me most. Yours isn't here? Drop it in the box and I'll dash back with an answer.

How do I put a deck into my game?

On a phone, tap Copy deck and Clash Royale opens with the deck ready to save. On a computer the key copies a link instead: send it to your phone and open it there.

Why are Evo Knight and Knight two different decks?

An evolution or a hero changes how the card plays, so the same 8 cards with a different evolution count as a different deck. Filter by card and pick Knight to see both kinds.

What do the green and red edges mean?

With the games we have, a deck's real win rate could be a few points either way. Green means it's above 50% even at the low end, red means it's below 50% even at the high end, and no colour means it could go either way.

Can I change a deck before I copy it?

Yes: the pen on any deck opens it in the Deck Builder, where you can swap cards, pick evolutions and heroes, and copy your version into the game.
