How it's counted

KingEvery number here is a real ranked battle from the top 1,000 players, counted fresh every morning. No made-up decks in my kingdom. He he he he!

  1. Every morning we read the last 30 battles of each player in the Path of Legends top 1,000 and keep only the ranked 1v1 games played since the last count.
  2. Both decks in a battle count. A battle between two top 1,000 players shows up in both their logs but is counted once, and draws are left out.
  3. A deck is its 8 cards, and an evolution or hero counts as part of it: a deck with Evo Knight and the same deck with plain Knight are two decks.
  4. Numbers cover the last 7 days. A deck needs 50 games in that time to be listed, so a lucky 5-0 can't top the board.
  5. Win rate = wins out of games. Usage = the share of all decks played in those battles.
  6. The ± next to a win rate is its 95% margin: the fewer games, the wider it is. "Winning" means even the low end of that range is above 50%, "Losing" means even the high end is below it, and anything in between is a coin flip.
  7. Deck names follow the win condition, the way players say them: "2.6 Hog", "3.0 X-Bow", "Giant Graveyard". The number is the deck's average elixir.
