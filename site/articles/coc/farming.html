The Perfect Base Is a Trap

Most players hunt for the perfect base. A minute of searching, two minutes of attacking, one raid done.

Your storages don't care how good the loot looked. They care how fast it comes in. Not the biggest raid. The fastest hour.

Quick Start

1. Fill your army camps with Valkyries.

2. Attack any base in regular Battles.

3. At 50%, end the battle and go again.

Who This Is For

Players in a high league. Witch League is already good enough, with up to 250,000 of each resource per win. Below that, use Sneaky Goblins.

The numbers here are Town Hall 18 maximums. At lower Town Halls everything is smaller, but the method is the same.

Big Raids or Fast Raids?

Fast raids come from mass Valkyries: about 20 seconds each, with no searching. Big raids come from Sneaky Goblins: a minute or two each, plus the search. The cheat sheet picks yours.

The Real Secret: Take Half and Run

Don't clear the base. Reach 50% and leave.

50% destruction earns one star, and one star counts as a win. A win pays the league bonus, and past 50% each extra second earns less than starting the next attack.

ValkyrieLog LauncherEarthquake Spell

Not a full clear. A fast 50%. Once your drop is clean, aim for about three attacks a minute.

Run it in regular Battles only. They are unlimited, and the league bonus applies there too. Ranked Battles cap your attacks each week.

One Troop. That's the Whole Army.

Fill every army camp with Valkyries, about 40 at Town Hall 18. They hit every building around them in a circle, so a ring of them chews through a base from all sides.

There are no training costs anymore, so every bit of loot is profit.

Everything else is temporary and optional. It only gets you to 50% faster:

  • Log Launcher: a siege machine that rolls logs through the outer layer of the base.
  • Earthquake Spells: in every slot, 11 at Town Hall 18. They crack walls open so the Valkyries reach buildings sooner.
  • Heroes: King, Queen, Warden and Royal Champion, geared for damage.

Build it once in the army maker and share the link with your clan.

Drop, Boost, Quit: The Seven Steps

Only step 2 is required. Steps 3 to 6 are boosters that bring 50% sooner.

  1. Skip the scouting. Attack the first base you get.
  2. Drop the Valkyries first. Use two fingers: start at the top and drag both down each side to ring the base.
  3. Optional: place the Log Launcher on one side with 3 Earthquake Spells in front of it.
  4. Optional: drop 4 Earthquake Spells where the King will enter and 4 where the Queen will enter.
  5. Optional: drop the King and Queen at the openings, then use the Queen's ability to shoot through the base.
  6. Optional: drop the Grand Warden on one side and the Royal Champion on the other.
  7. Use the King's ability if he is out, wait for 50% and end the battle.

No Heroes? No Problem.

Heroes are temporary here. You drop them to reach 50% faster, then end the battle. If they are upgrading, attack without them. Valkyries alone still get there.

If you use heroes, give them burst gear, not tanky gear. Hero equipment is gear attached to a hero for extra abilities. The Rage Gem is the best piece: it makes your troops destroy buildings faster, which is the point of the whole attack.

Seconds Are Loot

A clean attack takes about 20 seconds from one base to the next, so you land about three a minute.

  • Drop it the same way every time. Once your fingers know it, you can do it nearly blind.
  • Closed outer walls cost time. Spend the Earthquakes, take what the Valkyries give you and move on.
  • End the battle the second the bar hits 50%. Not one second later.

That speed fills storages. About 20 minutes fills 30 million of gold and 30 million of elixir:

Missed 50%? End It and Go Again

Don't stay and fight. If the Valkyries die or the attack drags, end the battle and start the next one. A bad base costs more time than it pays.

If it keeps happening, add the optional boosters: Earthquake Spells for the walls, then heroes with Rage Gem and Fireball.

Low League? Send the Sneaky Goblins

When the league bonus is small, or when you want the biggest single raid.

Sneaky GoblinSuper Wall BreakerJump SpellInvisibility Spell

Sneaky Goblins steal from everything that holds loot, from Gold Mines to the Town Hall. Super Wall Breakers open the compartments, Jump Spells lift them over walls, and the Invisibility Spell hides them from defenses.

Search for a base with 1 million or more sitting out. A good raid pays 800,000 or more of both Gold and Elixir, about 1.6 million in total. Expect one to two minutes per attack. They need no heroes and work from Town Hall 11 to 18.

Dark Elixir on Demand

Heroes eat Dark Elixir, and the Valkyrie method already brings it: about 1,600 per win from Witch League, up to 3,000 in Legend. On average that is 6,000 to 10,000 Dark Elixir every minute.

Want more? Aim the same attack at it. The Valkyries take the Dark Elixir Drills, the buildings that produce it, on the outside of the base. The storages usually sit in the core, where hero equipment comes in:

Rocket BackpackGiant ArrowFireball
  • Rocket Backpack or Giant Arrow: reach the storages in the middle of the base.
  • Fireball: works too, sometimes, and makes the attack faster.

Spells can't do this job. Lightning and Earthquake Spells can't hit resource buildings, so you need a hero with one of these pieces. Not stars. Dark Elixir per minute.

Fill Up Before Hammer Jam

Hammer Jam halves the cost of upgrades you start during the event, so your storages will drain fast. Farm before it starts. Expected: early November 2026.

The Five-Step Daily Loop

  1. Collect and queue every upgrade that is ready, heroes first.
  2. Run Valkyrie attacks until the storages you need are full.
  3. Spend the loot on walls and your longest upgrades.
  4. Run a few more attacks to refill what you spent.
  5. Before logging off, put the loot into upgrades so your storages have room for next time.

Stop Wasting Attacks Like This

  • Searching for a better base. The search costs more than the extra loot pays.
  • Clearing the whole base, or staying in a bad one. Take 50% or end it and start again.
  • Using Valkyries in a low league. The bonus is too small. Use Sneaky Goblins.
  • Farming into full storages. Loot over the cap is lost. Spend first, then farm.

FAQ

What is the fastest way to farm in Clash of Clans?

In a high league, drop mass Valkyries, take 50% and end the battle. Repeat on every base. In a low league, Sneaky Goblins farm better.

How do you farm gold and elixir with Valkyries in Clash of Clans?

Fill your camps with Valkyries, ring the base, and end the battle at 50%. The league bonus pays on top of the loot you steal. Earthquake Spells and heroes help but are not required.

Are Sneaky Goblins still good for farming in Clash of Clans?

Yes. They steal from collectors, storages and the Town Hall without heroes, and work from Town Hall 11 to 18. They pay more per attack than Valkyries but are slower per hour.

Summary
  • The secret: don't clear the base. Take 50% and leave. One star is a win, and a win pays the league bonus.
  • The army: Valkyries only, about 40 at Town Hall 18. Heroes, Earthquakes and the Log Launcher are optional. There are no training costs.
  • Heroes are temporary, and no heroes is not a problem. The Rage Gem is the best equipment piece.
  • Witch League is already good enough. The bonus grows to 410,000 of each plus 3,000 Dark Elixir at Legend.
  • About 20 seconds per attack fills 30 million of each in about 20 minutes. For extra Dark Elixir, add Rocket Backpack or Giant Arrow for the core storages.
  • In a low league, use Sneaky Goblins. Farm before Hammer Jam. Expected: early November 2026.

Storages don't fill themselves. Fifty percent, quit, repeat.
