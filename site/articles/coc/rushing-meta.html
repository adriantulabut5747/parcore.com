Max less. Reach the top sooner.

Why Rushing Is the Meta Now

Maxing every Town Hall takes years. The best troops, equipment and rewards wait at the top.

About 40% of the total time to max an account sits in TH17 and TH18. Stay low and you spend years avoiding nearly half the game.

Not “finish everything first.” Reach the top, then do the long upgrades.

Magic Items Pay Off at the Top

A Book or Hammer finishes one upgrade. The longer the upgrade, the more time it saves.

Save them for TH15+ upgrades. Spent on short upgrades at low Town Halls, the same item is worth a fraction as much.

Potions Cover Your Weak Spots

A rushed hero is a low-level hero. Potions fix that.

  • Hero Potion: raises your heroes and pets to the max level for your Town Hall.
  • Power Potion: does the same for your troops and spells.

Buy both with Raid Medals every week and use them in clan wars. Not low-level heroes. Max-level heroes, when it counts.

More Ore, Better Tools

Higher Town Halls start in higher leagues, and higher leagues pay more ore from the daily star bonus. At TH18 you can never fall below Titan 26.

The Blacksmith also unlocks gear as you climb. Ore is scarce, so you want the good pieces early.

Rage GemHealing TomeSpirit Fox

Rage Gem arrives at TH11 and Healing Tome at TH13. Pets start at TH14, and the Spirit Fox joins at TH16.

Why Weak Defenses Cost You Little

Equipment and defense nerfs changed the game. Until the latest Town Hall, a base with every defense built is only slightly stronger than one that has been maxed for years.

TH18 is the exception. The gap is big there. Rush up first, then fix your key defenses once you arrive.

The Real Secret: Strategic Rush, Not Bum Rush

A bum rush upgrades only what the next Town Hall requires. A strategic rush upgrades your offense and a few key defenses first.

Not “skip everything.” Skip the right things.

Merge Requirements You Can’t Skip

Three defenses must be maxed even on a rush, because every new Town Hall asks you to merge them. No merge, no upgrade.

Town HallMerged DefenseWhat You Need
TH17Ricochet CannonTwo max-level Cannons (level 21)
TH17Multi-Archer TowerTwo max-level Archer Towers (level 21)
TH18Multi-Gear TowerA geared up Cannon and a geared up Archer Tower, both level 21
NextSuper Wizard TowerTwo max-level Wizard Towers (level 17)

Max your Cannons and Archer Towers before TH16, and your Wizard Towers before TH18. The Multi-Gear Tower is the tricky one: gear ups need Builder Base progress, a Double Cannon at level 4 and an Archer Tower at level 6.

Expected: the Super Wizard Tower will be required for the next Town Hall too. Every merge before it has been.

How to Rush: The Upgrade Order

Offense → hero equipment → key defenses → the next merge. Laboratory first, always. Skip walls.

Key defenses: Eagle Artillery, Monolith, Spell Towers and the Revenge Tower. Everything else can sit at a low level. The full order for every Town Hall is in Stop Upgrading Blind.

Offense and equipment make you stronger. Walls only make your storages emptier.

Should You Rush to TH18?

Yes. Every player who can should rush to the latest Town Hall. The tools, ores and magic item value all sit up there.

What You Give UpHow to Handle It

Ranked pressureYou can never fall below your Town Hall’s league floor.

Stop Rushing Like This

  • Bum rushing. Upgrading only what the Town Hall asks for leaves your attacks weak.
  • Skipping the merge defenses. Unmaxed Cannons, Archer Towers or Wizard Towers block your next Town Hall.
  • Ignoring the Builder Base. The Multi-Gear Tower needs gear ups, and gear ups need Builder Base levels.
  • Wasting Books and Hammers. Keep them for TH15+ upgrades.
  • Upgrading walls. They are not worth it while you rush.
  • Forgetting your weekly potions. Buy both with Raid Medals every week.

FAQ

Is rushing worth it in Clash of Clans?

Yes. Rushing to the latest Town Hall gets you the best troops, equipment and ore income sooner, and Hero and Power Potions cover your low levels in war.

What do you need to unlock Town Hall 17 and 18 in Clash of Clans?

You need max-level Cannons and Archer Towers to merge for TH17, then a geared up Cannon and Archer Tower merged into a Multi-Gear Tower for TH18.

How do rushed players attack with low hero levels?

They use the Hero Potion for heroes and pets and the Power Potion for troops and spells. Both bring everything to the max level for your Town Hall.
