7 Reasons to Rush, Ranked

Maxing every Town Hall before moving up takes years, and the strongest troops, the best equipment and the biggest rewards are only at the top. These are the reasons to move up sooner, most important first.

  1. The longest upgrades start sooner. About 40% of the total upgrade time for a whole account is in TH17 and TH18 alone. The sooner you reach them, the sooner those upgrades start and the sooner they finish.
  2. Your offense gets stronger sooner. Every new Town Hall unlocks new troops, spells, hero levels and pets. A stronger offense wins more attacks in multiplayer, clan war and CWL, and more war wins earn your clan more rewards.
  3. You earn more ore. A higher Town Hall raises your league floor, and higher leagues pay more ore from the daily star bonus.
  4. You unlock the equipment worth keeping. Higher Blacksmith levels unlock more equipment, so your ore goes into the pieces you will use for years instead of pieces you will replace.
  5. Books and Hammers save weeks instead of days. A magic item finishes one upgrade instantly, so it is worth the most on the long timers at TH15 and above.
  6. Boosts cover the levels you skipped. The Hero Potion, Power Potion and Mighty Morsel raise your army to the max level for your Town Hall for a short time, so a rushed account still attacks at full strength in war.
  7. You practice the meta armies sooner. The meta armies, the ones top players use most, are built around troops, spells and equipment that only unlock at high Town Halls. You learn an army by attacking with it, so rushing lets you practice the meta now instead of mastering armies you will soon outgrow.

So do not max every lower Town Hall first. Move up, start the long upgrades there, and catch up on the rest while they run.

Magic Items Pay Off at the Top

A Book or Hammer finishes one upgrade instantly, no matter how long that upgrade is. That means it saves the most time when you use it on the longest upgrades.

Save them for upgrades at TH15 and above, where single timers last one to two weeks. Used on a two-day upgrade at a low Town Hall, the same Book only saves you two days.

Three Boosts Cover Your Low Levels

When you rush, your heroes, troops and spells fall behind, because you move up before upgrading them. Three boosts raise them to full strength for a short time.

  • Hero Potion: raises your heroes and pets to the max level for your Town Hall.
  • Power Potion: does the same for your troops and spells.
  • Mighty Morsel: raises your heroes, pets and hero equipment to the max level for your Town Hall for your next 3 attacks.

Buy the Hero Potion and Power Potion with Raid Medals every week. Drink them right before your clan war attacks, so you attack with max-level heroes, pets, troops and spells in the battles that matter most.

The Mighty Morsel is the strongest of the three for a rushed account, because it also maxes your hero equipment. It works the same whether your heroes and equipment are at level 1 or close to max. Eat a Mighty Morsel and drink a Power Potion, and a TH18 with a level 1 offense attacks with the same heroes, pets, equipment, troops and spells as a fully maxed TH18. Save your Mighty Morsels for the attacks you most need to win.

More Ore, Better Equipment

Every Town Hall has a league floor, which is the lowest league you can drop to. Higher Town Halls have higher floors, and higher leagues pay more ore from the daily star bonus. At TH18 you can never drop below Titan 26, so you keep a high ore income even after losses.

Upgrading the Blacksmith unlocks more equipment and higher equipment levels as you climb. Epic equipment also comes from the Hero Journey and special events.

Ore is scarce, so spend it only on the pieces that carry attacks. Max the five S tier Commons first, because they never cost Starry Ore. Then put your Starry Ore into the three S tier Epics.

The Real Secret: Strategic Rush, Not Bum Rush

A bum rush means upgrading only what the next Town Hall requires and nothing else. Your attacks stay weak, so you lose wars and earn less loot. A strategic rush means upgrading your offense, your hero equipment and a few key defenses before you move up.

Both kinds of rush skip most upgrades. The difference is what gets skipped. A strategic rush skips walls and minor defenses, but never your offense.

Town Hall Requirements You Can’t Skip

Even on a rush, you cannot skip these requirements. Most of them are merges. A merge combines two defenses into one stronger defense. Both source buildings must be at their max level before you can merge them, and the Town Hall will not upgrade until every required merge is finished. Two more requirements are not merges. The Eagle Artillery and the Inferno Artillery must each be at their max level.

Checklist: Before You Upgrade to TH17

Work through this list at TH16. Steps 3, 4 and 5 are required for TH17. Steps 6 and 7 are not, but doing them now saves you weeks at TH17.

  1. Upgrade four Cannons to level 21. Start as soon as you reach TH16 and keep one builder on them, because each upgrade takes days.
  2. Upgrade four Archer Towers to level 21 the same way.
  3. Merge the four Cannons in pairs into two Ricochet Cannons.
  4. Merge the four Archer Towers in pairs into two Multi-Archer Towers.
  5. Upgrade the Eagle Artillery to its max level.
  6. Get one more Cannon and one more Archer Tower to level 21 and gear both up. These two become your Multi-Gear Tower at TH17, so you can merge them on your first day there.
  7. Do the Builder Base upgrades that gear ups need. The Cannon gear up needs a Double Cannon at level 4, and the Archer Tower gear up needs a Builder Base Archer Tower at level 6. The Builder Base has its own builders and timers, so start early.

Checklist: Before You Upgrade to TH18

Work through this list at TH17. Steps 1 and 2 are required for TH18. Step 3 is not, but it lets you build the Super Wizard Tower on your first day at TH18.

  1. Merge your geared up Cannon and geared up Archer Tower, both at level 21, into the Multi-Gear Tower.
  2. Upgrade the Inferno Artillery to its max level.
  3. Upgrade two Wizard Towers to level 17, ready to merge into the Super Wizard Tower at TH18.

How to Rush: The Upgrade Order

Follow this order at every Town Hall on the way up, and keep every builder busy.

  1. Laboratory. Keep a research running at all times.
  2. Clan Castle and Army Camps.
  3. Heroes. Keep one builder on a hero at all times.
  4. The Town Hall requirements from the two checklists above. Give them a builder as soon as your Town Hall allows the upgrades, so they never hold up your next Town Hall.
  5. Key defenses. At TH15 and TH16, Eagle Artillery → Monolith and Spell Towers. At TH17, the Town Hall weapon. At TH18, Logger → Longshot and Smasher → Revenge Tower and Super Wizard Tower.
  6. Everything else, with walls always last.

Hero equipment does not use a builder. Upgrade it with ore whenever you have enough.

Leave every defense that is not on this list at a low level until you reach TH18. Wall upgrades cost huge amounts of gold and elixir and add little defense, so they only slow your rush down.

Timing tip

Time your Town Hall upgrade to finish just before Hammer Jam starts. A new Town Hall unlocks a long list of upgrades, and every upgrade you start during Hammer Jam costs 50% less and takes 50% less time.

Should You Rush to TH18?

Yes, for most players. If you play for strong attacks, ore and the newest content, rushing gets you there years sooner.

Do not rush if you are in a competitive war clan that needs your base to hold on defense. A rushed base gives up stars easily, and many competitive war clans do not accept rushed accounts.

Stop Rushing Like This

  • Bum rushing. Upgrading only what the next Town Hall requires leaves your offense too weak to win attacks.
  • Leaving the merge buildings for last. Eight defenses at level 21 take weeks of builder time. Start them as soon as you reach TH16.
  • Forgetting the Town Hall requirements. An unfinished merge, or an Eagle Artillery or Inferno Artillery below max level, stops your Town Hall from upgrading.
  • Ignoring the Builder Base. Without the Builder Base upgrades you cannot gear up your Cannon and Archer Tower, so you cannot build the Multi-Gear Tower or reach TH18.
  • Wasting Books and Hammers. Using them on short upgrades at low Town Halls saves only days. Keep them for TH15+ upgrades that take weeks.
  • Upgrading walls. Walls cost a lot of gold and elixir and add little defense, so they slow your rush down.
  • Forgetting your weekly potions. Buy the Hero Potion and Power Potion with Raid Medals every week, so they are ready for war.

FAQ

Is rushing worth it in Clash of Clans?

Yes, for most players. Rushing to the latest Town Hall gets you the best troops, equipment and ore income sooner, and the Mighty Morsel, Hero Potion and Power Potion raise your low levels to max for your Town Hall during war attacks. Players in competitive war clans should not rush, because a rushed base gives up stars easily.

What do you need to unlock Town Hall 17 and 18 in Clash of Clans?

Before TH17, merge four level 21 Cannons into two Ricochet Cannons and four level 21 Archer Towers into two Multi-Archer Towers, and max the Eagle Artillery. Before TH18, merge a geared up Cannon and Archer Tower into a Multi-Gear Tower and max the Inferno Artillery.

How do rushed players attack with low hero levels?

They use the Hero Potion for heroes and pets, the Power Potion for troops and spells, and the Mighty Morsel for heroes, pets and hero equipment. Each one raises those to the max level for your Town Hall.
