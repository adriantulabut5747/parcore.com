SS Tier: The Best Troops

These four are here because they fit almost any attack, at almost any Town Hall.

Balloon

The most flexible troop in the game, and the one you’ll see in almost every attack. Bring Balloons anywhere: test loons, a full LavaLoon, or a few behind a ground push to take out the defenses your main army can’t reach.

  • Works in both air and ground attacks.
  • Cheap and great at taking out defenses.
  • Doubles as test loons to find traps and Air Sweeper angles.
  • The core of every LavaLoon attack.

Healer

Healers make almost every ground attack better. Put them behind a Queen, Throwers or Hog Riders and the push simply refuses to die.

  • Fits every ground army you can build.
  • Heals in an area, so the whole group stays alive together.
  • Strong at every Town Hall.
  • Air Defenses and Seeking Air Mines wipe them out if you drop them in the wrong spot.

Dragon

Dragons are strong on their own. They don’t need a perfect army around them, and they stay consistent from the Town Hall you unlock them to the last.

  • Easy to use and reliable at every Town Hall.
  • Strong on its own, without a special army around it.
  • High health and splash damage clear whole clusters of buildings.

Thrower

Pair Throwers with Healers and they’re close to unkillable. They hit from long range, over walls, while the Healers keep them topped up.

  • Long range lets them hit defenses from behind walls.
  • Pairs with Healers better than any other troop.
  • Doesn’t need much spell support.
  • Without Healers and the Grand Warden, they go down fast.

AA Tier: Great Picks

Strong in most armies. Leave them out and you’ll feel it.

Super Wall Breaker

Most ground attacks can’t get in without them. They open the walls your army needs, and nothing does that job better.

  • One blast opens walls that would take several regular Wall Breakers.
  • Lets your ground army go exactly where you want it.

Headhunter

Attack without Headhunters and the enemy heroes become your biggest problem. They shut down the Queen, King and Warden fast.

  • Built to kill heroes, the defense that ruins most attacks.
  • Keeps enemy heroes from wiping out your funnel.
  • Very little health, and traps take them out quickly.

Rocket Balloon

Rocket Balloons hit hard and fast. They’re the best pick for taking out one key defense before the main attack lands.

  • Fast, with a huge first hit.
  • Great for sniping defenses, especially during a Warden walk.
  • Air Defenses and Seeking Air Mines still take it down fast.

Dragon Rider

Dragon Riders go straight for defenses and hit hard. A few of them can take out the core while the rest of the army cleans up.

  • Only targets defenses, so no damage is wasted.
  • Hits hard enough that a few of them can take out the core.
  • Dies easily, and the group splits apart instead of moving together like Dragons.

Super Bowler

Super Bowlers flatten a whole lane. Their boulders bounce through several buildings in a row.

  • Huge splash damage against tight bases.
  • Flattens a whole lane in one push.
  • Needs Healers and the Grand Warden, and struggles when it gets stuck on walls.

Super Yeti

Super Yetis tank like a Golem and still hit hard. You get a tank and a damage dealer in one troop.

  • Drops into the core from a Battle Blimp, and the mites it spawns tear the core apart.
  • Big health and real damage together.
  • Can be the main tank of a ground push.
  • Needs Healers and the Grand Warden behind it.

BB Tier: Good Picks

Great when the army is built around them. Weak when it isn’t.

Ice Golem

The best small tank in the game. When it dies it freezes everything around it, which buys your army a few free seconds.

  • The death freeze stops a whole cluster of defenses.
  • Also good in Clan Castles.
  • Can’t tank for long.
  • Spring Traps are its biggest weakness.

Super Minion

Super Minions hit from long range, so they can pick off defenses from a safe spot.

  • Long range keeps them out of trouble.
  • Also strong in Clan Castles.
  • Most of the time it attacks trash buildings instead of defenses.

Root Rider

Root Riders are huge ground tanks that break walls as they walk. Strong, but the rest of the army has to keep up.

  • Tanks and opens walls at the same time.
  • Slow, and she can’t carry an attack alone.
  • Doesn’t deal enough damage.

Inferno Dragon

Inferno Dragons melt anything with big health, like heroes and Town Halls. The beam gets stronger the longer it stays on one target.

  • Melts the tankiest buildings, like storages and, most importantly, Guardians.
  • Every target switch resets the beam, so swarms and small buildings waste it.

Furnace

Furnaces keep Firemites coming, so defenses always have something to shoot at besides your main army.

  • A steady stream of distractions.
  • Good for funneling.
  • Needs the push to stay together to be worth the space.

Super Witch

Her Big Boy is a huge tank that soaks hits while she summons the next one.

  • Summons a tank on demand.
  • Slow, so running out of time is her biggest problem.

Super Dragon

Super Dragons hit everything in a wide area. A few of them do a lot of damage.

  • Big splash and big health.
  • Huge housing cost and a Super Troop slot.
  • Very slow.

Electro Dragon

Electro Dragons chain lightning through tight bases and hit everything nearby when they die.

  • Chain damage tears through compact bases.
  • Easy to use.
  • Slow, and spread-out bases break the chain.
  • Weak against single-target Infernos and the Monolith.

CC Tier: Situational Picks

They still work. Another troop just does the same job better.

Baby Dragon

Great for funneling. On its own it gets angry and hits harder, but in a crowd it’s just a small dragon.

  • Cheap and easy funneling.
  • Clears big groups of Clan Castle troops, like a castle full of Witches.
  • Weak once it’s grouped with other troops.

Yeti

A solid tank that spills Yetimites as it takes damage. Newer tanks do the job better.

  • Good for funneling.
  • Can lead a Warden walk to where you want it to go.
  • Healers sometimes heal the Yeti instead of your hero charge.

Witch

Witches keep summoning skeletons that swarm single-target defenses, but only when you bring a lot of them.

  • Her skeletons keep defenses busy.
  • Weak against Giant Bombs.
  • Weak once all her skeletons are dead.
  • Only strong in numbers: a few Witches on their own do little.

Meteor Golem

Big health and splash pressure, but it eats a lot of space for what it does.

  • Very hard to kill.
  • Too much housing space for its damage.
  • Only unlocks at TH17, where better meta troops are available.

Hog Rider

Hogs jump walls and go straight for defenses. They don’t need Healers, but every spell has to land at the right moment.

  • Fast and only targets defenses.
  • Needs perfect spell timing to survive.
  • Weak against the Town Hall’s destruction blast and Mega Mines.

Miner

Miners travel underground past walls and traps, then pop up at defenses. Like Hogs, they live or die by your spell timing.

  • Walls don’t slow them down.
  • Needs perfect spell timing to survive.
  • Weak against the Town Hall’s destruction blast and Mega Mines.

Super Barbarian

Fast and tough for a Barbarian, and good for quick funnels.

  • Great for fast funneling, so your main troops stay inside the base.
  • Only good for funneling, and it costs a Super Troop slot.

Electro Titan

Tanky, with an aura that hits everything near her.

  • Strong against swarms and small troops.
  • Slow, and she takes a big chunk of your army.
  • Can get stuck on walls.

Lava Hound

The air tank that holds the Air Defenses while your Balloons work.

  • Soaks Air Defense fire, then splits into Lava Pups.
  • Almost no damage, so it needs a strong follow-up.

DD Tier: Below Average

You already have something better for each of these jobs.

Archer

Cheap and useful for funneling, but every army has something better for the job.

  • Cheap, with range.
  • Good for clearing the edges to funnel.
  • Dies to any splash damage.

Minion

Cheap air cleanup that rarely earns a slot.

  • Flies over walls and ground traps.
  • Quick cleanup at the edges of a base.
  • Very little health.
  • Too weak to earn a real slot in the army.

Apprentice Warden

He keeps troops alive, but he is support, not damage.

  • His aura helps nearby troops survive.
  • Barely deals damage.
  • Has to stay right next to the troops he protects.

Druid

Two forms, and neither is strong enough to stand out.

  • Heals nearby troops.
  • Turns into a bear and keeps fighting.
  • Neither form is strong enough to carry a push.
  • Healers do the healing job better.

Super Archer

Great in one setup, weak in every other.

  • Very strong from a Battle Blimp.
  • Long range, and her arrows pierce through buildings.
  • Weak outside a Blimp attack.
  • Takes a Super Troop slot.

Super Wizard

Strong on paper, but he rarely lives long enough to prove it.

  • Chain damage hits several buildings at once.
  • Fragile, so he often dies before the chain pays off.
  • Takes a Super Troop slot.

Valkyrie

Shreds packed bases, but only when the pathing is perfect.

  • Spins through packed buildings and troops.
  • Needs perfect pathing to be worth it.
  • Wastes time on walls when the path is bad.

Sneaky Goblin

A farming troop, not a war troop.

  • Invisible at the start, so it reaches storages untouched.
  • Fast loot for farming.
  • No use in war.
  • Takes a Super Troop slot.

Golem

All health and almost no damage.

  • Huge health.
  • Splits into Golemites when it dies.
  • Deals almost no damage.
  • Takes a lot of housing space.

Super Valkyrie

Her Rage on death is strong, but only if she falls in the right spot.

  • Drops a Rage Spell when she dies.
  • Strong splash damage.
  • Has to die in the right spot for the Rage to matter.
  • Takes a Super Troop slot.

EE Tier: Weak Picks

Each one has a use. It’s just too small.

Barbarian

Cheap filler and nothing more.

  • Cheap filler.
  • No real job in a modern army.
  • Dies to any splash damage.

Wizard

Big damage on a body that can’t survive splash.

  • High damage for its housing space.
  • Very fragile.
  • Any splash damage melts it.

Ruin Witch

Too much housing space for what she brings.

  • Adds pressure to a ground push.
  • Takes a lot of housing space for what she adds.
  • Needs the push to stay together.

Super Hog Rider

A bigger Hog that doesn’t pay for its extra space.

  • Tankier than a normal Hog Rider.
  • Costs more housing space than a normal Hog.
  • Less reliable, and it takes a Super Troop slot.

Ice Hound

A tank with a freeze, but it costs too much space.

  • Tanks the Air Defenses.
  • Freezes nearby defenses when it falls.
  • Costs a lot of housing space.
  • Deals almost no damage.

P.E.K.K.A

Hits like a truck, walks like a snail.

  • Huge single-target damage.
  • Very tanky.
  • Very slow.
  • Gets pulled anywhere by buildings and troops.

Wall Breaker

The Super version made it obsolete.

  • Cheap.
  • Splash kills them before they reach the wall.
  • Super Wall Breakers do the job far better.

FF Tier: The Weakest Troops

Another troop does every one of these jobs better.

Goblin

A loot troop that Sneaky Goblins replaced.

  • Fast, and goes straight for resources.
  • Very fragile.
  • Sneaky Goblins do the same job better.

Giant

The first tank you unlock, and the first one you replace.

  • A cheap tank at low Town Halls.
  • Barely deals damage.
  • Every tank you unlock after it does more.

Super Giant

Not worth a Super Troop slot.

  • Tankier than a Giant.
  • Barely hits back.
  • Takes a Super Troop slot.

Super Miner

Not worth a Super Troop slot.

  • Moves underground past walls.
  • Slow to get going.
  • Takes a Super Troop slot.

Bowler

The Super version took its job.

  • Its boulders bounce through two targets.
  • Gets stuck on walls most of the time.
  • Fragile without support.

The Real Secret: Flexibility Beats Raw Power

Look at the top of the list. Balloons, Healers, Dragons and Throwers aren’t there because they hit the hardest. They’re there because they work in almost any army, at almost any Town Hall, against almost any base.

A troop that only shines in one perfect setup drops down the list, no matter how strong it is in that setup. When you build an army, pick troops that still work when the attack doesn’t go to plan.

FAQ

What is the best troop in Clash of Clans?

The Balloon. It’s the most flexible troop in the game and fits almost every attack, air or ground. Healers, Dragons and Throwers sit right beside it in S tier.

Why are Healers so good in Clash of Clans?

They fit every ground army. Behind a Queen or a group of Throwers, they keep the push alive long enough to finish the base. Air Defenses and Seeking Air Mines are the only real threat.

Are Super Troops worth it in Clash of Clans?

Some are. Super Wall Breakers, Rocket Balloons, Super Bowlers and Super Yetis are A tier. Super Giants and Super Miners are not worth the boost.

Summary
  • S tier is Balloon, Healer, Dragon and Thrower, because they work in almost any army.
  • Throwers behind Healers are close to unkillable.
  • Balloons are the most flexible troop: test loons, LavaLoon, or support for a ground push.
  • Without Headhunters, enemy heroes wreck your attack. Without Super Wall Breakers, most ground armies can’t get in.
  • B and C troops are strong only when the army is built around them.
  • D to F troops are outclassed by something you already have.
  • Flexibility beats raw power: pick troops that still work when the attack goes wrong.
