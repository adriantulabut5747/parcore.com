Clash of Clans Hammer Jam Explained: What to Upgrade While It's Cheap

Half the price, double the gains.

What Is Hammer Jam?

Hammer Jam is a limited-time Clash of Clans event that cuts Home Village upgrade costs and upgrade times by 50%. Recent runs have lasted about two and a half weeks.

The first Hammer Jams only cut costs. Recent ones also halve upgrade times and boost your collectors: usually 4× production and capacity, sometimes 3× or 2×.

The capacity boost works for attackers too. Every player's collectors hold far more than usual, so full collectors on other bases are worth hitting. Raiding them is one of the fastest ways to fund your upgrades during the event.

When to Expect Hammer Jam

Hammer Jam usually arrives just before a new Town Hall level, which is why most runs land in November. The last three years all had a November run, and 2026 also had one in May.

Expected: early November 2026. Supercell has not announced the date yet, so get ready before then.

Get Ready Before It Starts

  • Fill every storage. Every resource you hold when the event starts buys twice as much as it does today.
  • Stop starting long upgrades about a week before. A builder still busy with a full-price ten-day upgrade when the event starts misses most of the discount.
  • Stock up on Builder Potions. Spend your Clan War League medals in the League Shop on as many Builder Potions as you can. See the real secret below.
  • Save Books and Hammers for after the event. They finish one upgrade, and during Hammer Jam that upgrade is already half as long. They are worth twice as much once prices are back to normal.
  • Plan your builder queue now. Write down your five longest upgrades so every builder starts one in the first hour of the event.

What to Upgrade First

The discount is a percentage, so the biggest savings come from the most expensive and slowest upgrades. Fifty percent off a two-hour upgrade saves one hour. Fifty percent off a ten-day upgrade saves five days. Put every builder but one on a long upgrade first and keep the cheap ones for last.

  1. Heroes. They are the longest upgrades in the game and they cost Dark Elixir, the resource you farm slowest. Half the time also means your heroes are back for war sooner.
  2. Laboratory, then your longest research. If a Laboratory upgrade is due, do it first. The higher level unlocks research that also gets the discount.
  3. The newest defenses at your Town Hall level. They cost the most and take the longest of all your buildings.
  4. Clan Castle and army buildings that are holding back your attacks.
  5. Walls, all event long. See the next section.

Upgrading the Town Hall itself is worth it only if the rest of your base is maxed. If it is not, the discount pushes you up a level before your army and defenses are ready, and you end up facing stronger bases with a weaker army.

The Real Secret: Builder Potions

This is the move most players miss. A Builder Potion makes every busy builder work much faster for an hour. During Hammer Jam your upgrades finish sooner, your builders free up sooner, and every new upgrade they start is half price too.

More upgrades started during the event means more upgrades at 50% off. Builder Potions are how you fit more of them into the same two and a half weeks.

Stock up now

Before Hammer Jam starts, spend your Clan War League medals in the League Shop on Builder Potions, as many as you can buy. Do not buy Hammers there. A Hammer finishes one upgrade, and during the event that upgrade is already half as long. A potion speeds up every builder at once.

Drink a potion when every builder is busy on a long upgrade. A potion does nothing for an idle builder, so don't use one while you're still placing upgrades.

Keep One Builder Free for Walls

Walls upgrade instantly, but only while a builder is free. If every builder is busy, the loot you farm has nowhere to go, and once your storages are full the loot from every extra attack is lost.

During Hammer Jam, keep one builder free for the whole event. Farm, and whenever your storages get close to full, spend the Gold and Elixir on walls. Every attack then turns into wall levels instead of overflowing your storages.

Rule of thumb

Never let a storage sit full. The wall calculator shows how many walls your storages can pay for right now.

Push for Your 6th Builder

The 6th builder is unlocked through Builder Base progress, and Builder Base upgrades are 50% off during Hammer Jam too. If you have been putting it off, this is the cheapest and fastest time to push your Builder Base and get that extra builder.

An extra builder pays for itself during the event: one more long upgrade running at half price, every day Hammer Jam is on.

Hammer Jam and the Gold Pass

The Gold Pass builder and research boosts stack with Hammer Jam. They multiply rather than add. With a 20% Gold Pass boost, the event takes 50% off first and the Gold Pass takes 20% off what is left, so you pay 40% of the normal price. That is 60% off, not 70%.

The wall calculator does this math for you: turn on Hammer Jam and pick your Gold Pass level.

Mistakes That Waste the Event

  • Leaving builders idle overnight. Start your longest upgrades before you go to sleep, on every builder except the one you keep for walls.
  • Spending the event on cheap upgrades. The hour you save on a short upgrade is nothing next to the days you save on a long one.
  • Buying Hammers in the League Shop. Builder Potions do far more during Hammer Jam. Buy those instead.
  • Using Books and Hammers during the event. A Book of Building skips half as much time during Hammer Jam as it does afterwards.
  • Jumping Town Hall early. A discounted Town Hall upgrade is still a rush if your heroes and defenses are behind.
Summary
  • Everything is 50% off, cost and time: buildings, Laboratory, heroes, pets, walls and the Builder Base. Hero Equipment isn't.
  • The discount counts for upgrades you start during the event, even if they finish after it.
  • Start your longest upgrades first and keep one builder free for walls.
  • Builder Potions are the secret: buy them with League Medals, never Hammers, and drink them while every builder is busy.
  • Save Books and Hammers for after the event.
  • Collectors run at up to 4× and hold far more, so full collectors on other bases are worth raiding.
  • Gold Pass stacks: 50% + 20% = 60% off, not 70%.
  • Expected: early November 2026.
