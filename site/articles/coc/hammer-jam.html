What Is Hammer Jam?

Hammer Jam is a limited-time Clash of Clans event that cuts Home Village upgrade costs and upgrade times by 50%. Recent runs have lasted about two and a half weeks.

Hammer Jam’s bonuses have changed over time. Early runs reduced upgrade costs only; later runs also cut upgrade times by 50%. Collector boosts vary by event too: collectors may produce and hold 2×, 3×, or 4× their normal amount. Check the event announcement for the current bonuses.

The capacity boost works for attackers too. Every player's collectors hold far more than usual, so full collectors on other bases are worth hitting. Raiding them is one of the fastest ways to fund your upgrades during the event.

When to Expect Hammer Jam

Hammer Jam often arrives ahead of a new Town Hall, usually around November. The May 2026 event was Builder Base Bonanza, not Hammer Jam. Supercell also ran a separate Summer Jam in July 2026: its final phase discounted all Home Village upgrades. Check each event announcement for its dates and eligible upgrades; their bonuses are different.

Expected: early November 2026. Supercell has not announced the date yet, so get ready before then.

Get Ready Before It Starts

  • Fill every storage. Every resource you hold when the event starts buys twice as much as it does today.
  • Stop starting long upgrades about a week before. The discount only counts for upgrades you start during the event. An upgrade already running when Hammer Jam begins keeps its full price and full timer, and its builder stays tied up while everyone else is upgrading at half price.
  • Time your Town Hall upgrade. If you want its temporary boosts during Hammer Jam, plan for the upgrade to finish as the event begins. Start it as many days ahead as its timer shows; for a 14-day Town Hall upgrade, start 14 days before. Starting it during Hammer Jam discounts the timer, but delays the new Town Hall and its boosts until the upgrade finishes. For example, reaching TH15 currently gives 5 days of Power, Resource, Hero, and Star Bonus boosts—not 7.
  • Stock up on Builder Potions. Spend your Clan War League medals in the League Shop on as many Builder Potions as you can. See the real secret below.
  • Stock up on Research Potions. Spend your Raid Medals at the Trader on Research Potions. See Research Potions below.
  • Compare Books and Hammers before using them. Hammer Jam shortens upgrade timers, so an item finishes less remaining time than it would after the event. It can still be the right choice for a very long upgrade or an urgent finish.
  • Plan your builder queue now. Write down your five longest upgrades so every builder starts one in the first hour of the event.

What to Upgrade First

Hammer Jam removes half of an upgrade’s timer: a two-hour upgrade takes one hour, while a ten-day upgrade takes five days. Longer upgrades save you more time. Start your longest upgrades first, then use shorter upgrades when a builder becomes free. When you are grinding walls, free up one builder for them.

  1. Heroes. They are the longest upgrades in the game and they cost Dark Elixir, the resource you farm slowest. Half the time also means your heroes are back for war sooner.
  2. Laboratory, then your longest research. If a Laboratory upgrade is due, do it first. The higher level unlocks research that also gets the discount.
  3. The newest defenses at your Town Hall level. They cost the most and take the longest of all your buildings.
  4. Clan Castle and army buildings that are holding back your attacks.
  5. Walls, whenever your storages fill up. See Free a Builder When You Grind Walls.

The Real Secret: Builder Potions

This is the move most players miss. A Builder Potion speeds up every active Home Village builder by 10× for one hour. Each builder gets 10 hours of progress while one hour passes, so the potion removes 9 hours from each active builder's upgrade timer.

120-medal comparison: Four Builder Potions versus one Hammer of Building, using a 14-day upgrade (7 days during Hammer Jam). Each potion contributes 9 hours saved per active builder. The potion rows add those savings across all builders over all four potion hours; they are combined builder-hours, not elapsed clock time. The Hammer removes time from one upgrade.

Stock up now

Before Hammer Jam starts, spend your Clan War League medals in the League Shop on Builder Potions, as many as you can buy. With five builders busy, I recommend potions over a Hammer of Building: four potions match its 120-medal price and spread the boost across your whole upgrade queue. Save a Hammer for a building whose discounted timer is longer than 7 days 12 hours, or when you need one upgrade finished immediately.

Use a potion when every builder is busy on a long upgrade. A potion does nothing for an idle builder, so don't use one while you're still placing upgrades.

Research Potions for the Laboratory

Builder Potions don't touch the Laboratory. Its potion is the Research Potion: it speeds up research 24× for one hour, so the Laboratory gets 24 hours of progress while one hour passes.

During Hammer Jam that hour is worth twice as much. Research timers are already cut in half, so the 23 hours a potion removes cover twice as much research as they would after the event.

Spend your Raid Medals

Before the event, spend your Raid Medals at the Trader on Research Potions. Laboratory research and pet upgrades both get 50% off in cost and time, so keep the Laboratory and the Pet House busy all event.

Free a Builder When You Grind Walls

Walls upgrade instantly, but only while a builder is free. If every builder is busy, the loot you farm has nowhere to go, and once your storages are full the loot from every extra attack is lost.

Not for the whole event. A free builder is a builder not working on a discounted timed upgrade. Free one up only while you are grinding walls: when your heroes are busy on Dark Elixir and Gold and Elixir keep piling up. Spend it on walls before your storages fill, then put that builder back on a long upgrade.

Rule of thumb

Never let a storage sit full. The wall calculator shows how many walls your storages can pay for right now.

Push for Your 6th Builder

The 6th builder is unlocked through Builder Base progress, but Hammer Jam discounts Home Village upgrades only. Keep working toward B.O.B when you can; once unlocked, he adds another builder to your Home Village upgrade queue during the event.

Hammer Jam and the Gold Pass

The Gold Pass builder and research boosts stack with Hammer Jam. They multiply rather than add. With a 20% Gold Pass boost, the event takes 50% off first and the Gold Pass takes 20% off what is left, so you pay 40% of the normal price. That is 60% off, not 70%.

The wall calculator does this math for you: turn on Hammer Jam and pick your Gold Pass level.

Mistakes That Waste the Event

  • Leaving builders idle overnight. Start your longest upgrades before you go to sleep, on every builder. Only leave one free while you are grinding walls.
  • Spending the event on cheap upgrades. The hour you save on a short upgrade is nothing next to the days you save on a long one.
  • Buying a Hammer without comparing the timers. Four Builder Potions cost the same 120 League Medals. With all five builders working, they remove 180 hours of Hammer Jam timer time in total; compare that against the discounted timer on the building you'd hammer.
  • Using Books and Hammers during the event. A Book of Building skips half as much time during Hammer Jam as it does afterwards.
Summary
  • Home Village buildings, Laboratory research, heroes, pets and walls get 50% off in cost and time. Builder Base upgrades and Hero Equipment don't.
  • The discount counts for upgrades you start during the event, even if they finish after it.
  • Start your longest upgrades first. Free a builder for walls only while you are grinding them.
  • Builder Potions are strong value: four cost 120 League Medals and save 180 combined builder-hours with five active builders. A Hammer can be better for one longer upgrade or an urgent finish.
  • Compare a Book or Hammer's time saved against the potion total before spending it.
  • Spend your Raid Medals on Research Potions. Laboratory research and pet upgrades are 50% off in cost and time too.
  • Collectors run at up to 4× and hold far more, so full collectors on other bases are worth raiding.
  • Gold Pass stacks: 50% + 20% = 60% off, not 70%.
  • Expected: early November 2026.
