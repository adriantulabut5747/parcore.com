How Clan Games Points Work

Most players lose hours on Clan Games. The challenges aren't hard. The picks are bad, and the attacks keep running long after the challenge is done.

4,000 points is your target. Reach it and you earn one bonus reward, on top of your normal pick from every tier your clan unlocks. Your personal cap is 10,000, but points past 4,000 only help your clan.

Supercell shows the rewards about a day before the games start.

Know these rules before you start a challenge:

  • You need to be in a clan. Pick your clan before you start. Once you start a challenge, you can't earn Clan Games points for any other clan that season.
  • Every challenge has a time limit. Don't start one you can't finish in time.
  • Only one person can hold a challenge. Once a clanmate starts it, it's theirs. Pick another.
  • Expired challenges can be revived for gems. Worth it for a big one, like a 1,500-point Super Troop challenge.

The Real Secret: Points Per Attack

Most players take the biggest number on the board. Don't. Count the attacks.

A 400-point challenge that needs four attacks pays 100 per attack. A 300-point challenge you finish in one attack pays 300 per attack. Take the 300.

The 5-second check

Divide the points by the number of attacks. Aim for at least 150 points per attack. Below that, skip it.

Your goal is not a clean attack. Your goal is a finished challenge.

What Army Should You Use?

Mass Valkyries. They tear through outside buildings, which gets you the 50% you need. Every challenge needs at least one star, Add your heroes to make it even faster.

ValkyrieBarbarian KingArcher Queen

Leave a few slots open for the unit a challenge asks for. Need one Dragon? Replace a few Valkyries. Need one Jump Spell? Swap one spell. Don't rebuild your army for every challenge.

  • Heroes: equip for damage, not survival. Giant Arrow, Fireball and Rocket Backpack are good picks. Speed matters more than staying alive.
  • Create and save your army in the army maker and edit only the open slots.

How Do You Finish Attacks in Seconds?

Don't search. Don't plan. Drop and leave.

  1. Take the first base you see. Don't tap Next looking for a better one.
  2. Drop Valkyries on the outside edge, with a hero on each side.
  3. Add what the challenge asks for: the one troop or the one spell.
  4. Use hero abilities right away. Don't wait for the perfect moment.
  5. Leave as soon as the challenge completes and you have a star. 50% destruction or a destroyed Town Hall gives you one.

Some attacks last only a minute or two. That is normal. The rewards are worth more.

Do This Before Clan Games Start

  • Right before you start grinding, free one builder and empty your storages. Only one builder, and only for the grind, not for days. Loot challenges need room in your storages, so spend your gold and elixir on an upgrade or walls first. The wall calculator shows how many walls your storages can pay for.
  • Keep some Dark Elixir. Super Troop challenges pay 1,500 points, but they need the troop boosted, and boosting costs Dark Elixir.
  • Queue the next army the second an attack ends. Training time is the only thing that slows a fast attack.
  • Start on day one. Reach 4,000 early, then keep going to help your clan.

Your Clan Games Routine

  1. Open the board. Divide points by attacks for every challenge. Gold-marked ones go first.
  2. Start the best one. Swap in only the troop or spell it needs.
  3. Attack: drop, finish, leave.
  4. Queue the next army. Repeat until you reach 4,000.
  5. Check your clan's total. Keep going past 4,000 if you want to help your clan reach the next tier.
  6. When the games end, claim your rewards.

Which Rewards Should You Pick?

Time first. Resources last. Gold and Elixir come back from farming. Time doesn't.

  • Books and Hammers: they finish an upgrade on the spot. Save them for the long upgrades at Town Hall 15 and above.
  • Builder Potions: stock them for Hammer Jam and drink them while every builder is busy.
  • Hero Potion and Power Potion: worth it if you rush. They cover your low levels in war.
  • Gold, Elixir and Dark Elixir: take them only when your storages are empty and a big upgrade is waiting.

Your 4,000-point bonus pick can come from any tier your clan unlocked. Take the best item on the board, even if it sits in a lower tier.

Unclaimed rewards stay in the caravan for 7 days. After that, they are gone.

  • Check the clan total on day five. If a tier is within reach, ask who still has points left. Missing a tier by a few hundred points costs everyone its rewards and its XP.
  • Split the board. Every member sees the same challenges. Take the ones your army fits and leave the rest to clanmates.
  • Go past 4,000 to help your clan. Points above 4,000 don't earn you more rewards, but they push the clan toward the next tier, and everyone in the clan benefits.
  • Clan XP is paid to the clan when the games end. Every tier adds its XP, and a full Tier VI finish is worth over 400 in total.

Stop Wasting Clan Games Like This

  • Chasing the biggest number. Points per attack beats points per challenge.
  • Playing attacks to the end. Leave when the challenge is done.
  • Trashing and re-picking. Every trashed challenge costs a 10-minute wait. Choose first.
  • Starting with full storages. Loot challenges stall when the loot has nowhere to go.
  • Waiting for the last day. A new challenge can't be started in the final minute.
  • Forgetting to claim. Rewards expire after 7 days.

FAQ

How do you get 4,000 Clan Games points fast in Clash of Clans?

Pick Home Village challenges that pay at least 150 points per attack. Build a Valkyrie army, swap in the one troop or spell each challenge needs, and end every attack the moment the challenge completes.

What are the best Clan Games challenges to pick?

Single-troop and single-spell challenges, easy outside buildings, one-star challenges and gold-marked challenges. If a Super Troop challenge shows up, take it instantly: it pays 1,500 points. Skip Builder Base, core defenses and loot challenges while your storages are full.

How many Clan Games points do you need for the bonus reward?

4,000 points. It gives you one extra reward from any tier your clan has unlocked. Your personal cap is 10,000.
