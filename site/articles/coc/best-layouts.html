Town Hall 9 Layout

  • Hidden Teslas in the core. Tesla Towers wait in the middle, right beside the Clan Castle. The attack reaches the core, the Teslas appear, and a Furnace with 12 Archers floods out at the same moment.
  • The Town Hall is bait. This is an anti-3-star war base, so the Town Hall sits in a corner as a trash building. Giving up its star costs nothing. Giving up the third star costs the war.
  • No cheap air attack. Every Air Sweeper covers an air approach, and the four Air Defenses are spread far apart, so Lightning and Zap spells get almost no value on them.

Town Hall 10 Layout

  • Single-target Infernos, on purpose. Most TH10 war attacks are Dragons, and multi-target Infernos barely slow them down. Single-target Infernos burn through Dragons and, more importantly, heroes.
  • A corner Town Hall that breaks siege machines. Log Launchers and Wall Wreckers drive straight for the Town Hall. With the Town Hall on the corner edge, they head away from the core and open very few walls.
  • Both Infernos sit under Air Sweepers. Each Inferno Tower has an Air Sweeper covering it, so flying troops struggle to reach either one.
  • The Clan Castle sits between the Infernos. Its troops pull the attack in and tank while both Infernos fire freely. The Archer Queen guards the bottom Inferno.
  • No way in for a Queen Charge. The pathing gives a Queen Charge no clean route to either Inferno Tower. Both are very hard to reach.

Town Hall 11 Layout

  • Multi-target Infernos this time. At TH11 the multi-target Infernos deal enough damage to earn their place, so this base switches them on.
  • A corner Town Hall that breaks siege machines. A Log Launcher rolls toward the Town Hall. With the Town Hall in a corner, the Log misses the center and breaks very few walls. Angle it at the one weak spot and it runs into the defenses aimed right at it.
  • Every key defense fights alone. Core defenses and heroes are spread out evenly, so each one holds its own fight instead of falling in a chain.
  • Minion Prince on Inferno duty. He guards an Inferno Tower and adds both single-target and splash damage.
  • An Electro Titan in the Clan Castle. She is extremely tanky, and her splash shreds low-hitpoint troops: Hogs, Miners, Balloons and, above all, Witches, the most popular attack at TH11.

Town Hall 12 Layout

  • No core to aim at. There is no center to the base, so there is no straight path through it.
  • Every key defense has its own compartment. Each one is guarded by heroes and traps, so the attack has to take them one compartment at a time.
  • Dead zones break the funnel. Building a funnel here is very hard. Troops wander into the dead zones, and the attacker’s plan falls apart.

Town Hall 13 Layout

  • Three Infernos around the Clan Castle. Both multi-target Infernos cover one side. The other side gets the Town Hall and a single-target Inferno, so every side has both splash and single-target damage.
  • The Town Hall is the start of a trap. When the Town Hall falls, its blast damages and slows every troop around it. Their next target is the single-target Inferno.
  • Then the trap closes. A Tornado Trap guards that Inferno, and the Ice Golems from the Clan Castle freeze whatever it pulls in. Slowed, pulled together and frozen, the troops melt.

FAQ

How do I copy a Clash of Clans base layout?

Tap Copy Layout on the base’s picture. Clash of Clans opens with the layout on screen; tap Copy and pick a layout slot. The link only works at the same Town Hall level as the base.

What Clan Castle troops should I use for a war base?

It depends on the base. A tanky troop with splash damage, like the Electro Titan, ruins most TH10 and TH11 attacks. Each base on this page names the Clan Castle that suits it under its picture.

Which Town Hall levels have a base here?

Town Hall 9 to Town Hall 13 right now. The Town Hall 14 to 18 bases are on the way.

Summary
  • These are anti-3-star war bases: they protect the third star, not the Town Hall.
  • A Town Hall in the corner sends Log Launchers and Wall Wreckers away from the core.
  • TH10 runs single-target Infernos against Dragons and heroes; TH11 switches to multi-target.
  • An Electro Titan in the Clan Castle shreds Hogs, Miners, Balloons and Witches.
  • Spread-out Air Defenses give Lightning and Zap spells almost no value.
  • The TH13 base turns the Town Hall’s own blast into the start of a freeze trap.
  • A base’s copy link only works at the same Town Hall level as the base.
