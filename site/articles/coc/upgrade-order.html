The Rule: Offense, Defense, Walls

Every new Town Hall unlocks dozens of upgrades at once, and the order you take them in decides how fast your account grows. The most common mistake is upgrading whatever is cheapest. That leaves a base with strong Cannons and an army that can't win a war attack.

One order works at every Town Hall:

  1. Offense. The Laboratory first, then heroes, then offense buildings.
  2. Defense. The newest core defenses first, older defenses after.
  3. Walls. Last, with whatever loot is left over.

Your goal is not a base that looks maxed. Your goal is an army that wins. A stronger army wins more attacks, and those wins bring in the loot that pays for everything else.

The Real Secret: The Laboratory Never Sleeps

The Laboratory is the slowest part of any account's progress. It researches without using a builder, so every hour it sits idle is time you never get back.

  • Upgrade it first. Start the Laboratory upgrade as soon as your new Town Hall finishes. Every new research level depends on it. If you have a Hammer or Book of Building, use it here.
  • Keep it running. Start your longest research before you go offline. Save short research for when you can check back in.
What to research first

The troops you use most. If you attack with Dragons, upgrade Dragons first. If you attack on the ground, upgrade your main ground troops first.

Ground attackers: don't skip Super Wall Breakers. They are one of the most valuable upgrades you can make. Keep them at the same level as the walls you attack. At that level, a Super Wall Breaker still breaks the wall even if it is destroyed before it gets there.

Save boosts for long research. Research Potions and the Gold Pass research boost save hours on a long research and almost nothing on a short one.

The Pet House Is a Second Laboratory

The Pet House unlocks at Town Hall 14. Like the Laboratory, it upgrades pets without a builder, so keep it busy at all times.

  • Upgrade the Pet House right after the Laboratory. Each new level unlocks new pets.
  • Upgrade the pets you actually use. A pet only helps the hero it is assigned to.
  • Pets are offense. They come before any defense.

Should You Rush Town Hall?

It depends on how you play. Some players max every building before moving up. Others move up as soon as they can. Both work. Here is the trade-off:

Rushing: prosRushing: cons
New troops, spells and defenses arrive sooner.War and Clan War League match you against stronger bases.
Bigger storages let you afford bigger upgrades.Your base is easier to raid while it catches up.
A Hero Potion makes up for low hero levels in war.A Hero Potion is used up, so you need a new one for every war.
A Power Potion makes up for low troop levels in war.A Power Potion is used up the same way.

The best time to move up is just before Hammer Jam, when every upgrade at the new level is half price.

Whichever way you play, keep the Laboratory ahead. Your army is only as strong as your research.

Town Hall 3

The Laboratory unlocks at this level. Build it before anything else.

  1. Laboratory, then start researching right away.
  2. Offense buildings.
  3. Defenses: Mortar → Archer Towers and Cannons → traps.
  4. Walls.

Town Hall 4

Air Defense and Balloons unlock. Upgrades still take minutes at this stage, so move quickly.

  1. Laboratory.
  2. Offense buildings.
  3. Defenses: Air Defense → Mortar and Archer Towers → traps.
  4. Walls.

Town Hall 5

Spells unlock. Keep your offense moving right after the Laboratory.

  1. Laboratory.
  2. Offense buildings.
  3. Defenses: Wizard Tower → Air Defense → Mortars → traps.
  4. Walls.

Town Hall 6

Healers and the Rage Spell unlock. Together with Giants, they make your first strong attack.

  1. Laboratory.
  2. Offense buildings.
  3. Defenses: Air Sweeper → Wizard Towers → Air Defense → traps.
  4. Walls.

Town Hall 7

Your first hero and Dark Elixir unlock here. From this level on, upgrade order starts to matter.

  1. Laboratory.
  2. Dark Elixir Drill and Dark Elixir Storage. Heroes are paid for in Dark Elixir.
  3. Barbarian King. Keep him upgrading for the whole Town Hall.
  4. Offense buildings.
  5. Defenses: Air Defense → Hidden Tesla → other defenses → traps.
  6. Walls.

Town Hall 8

The Archer Queen and the Dark Spell Factory unlock. From now on, keep one builder on a hero at all times.

  1. Laboratory.
  2. Archer Queen, then keep both heroes upgrading.
  3. Offense buildings.
  4. Defenses: Air Defense → Bomb Tower → Wizard Towers → other defenses → traps.
  5. Walls.

Town Hall 9

The Minion Prince and X-Bows unlock. Hero levels now decide most war attacks.

  1. Laboratory.
  2. Minion Prince, then keep all three heroes upgrading.
  3. Offense buildings.
  4. Defenses: X-Bows → Air Defense and Wizard Towers → other defenses → traps.
  5. Walls.

Bases: TH9 layouts.

Town Hall 10

Inferno Towers and Miners unlock. Hero upgrades now take long enough to tie up builders, so plan around them.

  1. Laboratory.
  2. Heroes. Keep one builder on them at all times.
  3. Offense buildings.
  4. Defenses: Inferno Towers → X-Bows → other defenses → traps.
  5. Walls.

Bases: TH10 layouts.

Town Hall 11

The Grand Warden and the Eagle Artillery unlock. Each is the top priority on its side.

  1. Laboratory.
  2. Grand Warden, then the other heroes.
  3. Offense buildings.
  4. Defenses: Eagle Artillery → Inferno Towers → X-Bows and Air Defenses (equal priority) → other defenses → traps.
  5. Walls.

Bases: TH11 layouts.

Town Hall 12

Siege machines unlock and become part of every war attack.

  1. Laboratory.
  2. Heroes.
  3. Offense buildings.
  4. Defenses: Eagle Artillery → Inferno Towers → X-Bows and Air Defenses (equal priority) → other defenses → traps.
  5. Walls.

Bases: TH12 layouts.

Town Hall 13

The Royal Champion and the Scattershot unlock. With more heroes to upgrade, builders run short. Give heroes priority.

  1. Laboratory.
  2. Royal Champion, then the other heroes.
  3. Offense buildings.
  4. Defenses: Eagle Artillery → Inferno Towers → Scattershots → X-Bows and Air Defenses (equal priority) → other defenses → traps.
  5. Walls.

Bases: TH13 layouts.

Town Hall 14

The Pet House unlocks. Build it right away: like the Laboratory, it upgrades without a builder.

  1. Laboratory.
  2. Pet House. Keep it busy (see the Pet House section).
  3. Heroes.
  4. Offense buildings.
  5. Defenses: Eagle Artillery → Inferno Towers → Scattershots → X-Bows and Air Defenses (equal priority) → other defenses → traps.
  6. Walls.

Bases: TH14 layouts.

Town Hall 15

New pets, the Monolith and the Spell Tower unlock. Upgrade the Pet House early, since the new pets need it.

  1. Laboratory.
  2. Pet House.
  3. Heroes.
  4. Offense buildings.
  5. Defenses: Eagle Artillery → Monolith and Spell Towers → Inferno Towers → Scattershots → X-Bows and Air Defenses (equal priority) → other defenses → traps.
  6. Walls.

Bases: TH15 layouts.

Town Hall 16

Upgrade times are now long enough that one idle builder overnight costs you days. Start the longest upgrades before you log off.

  1. Laboratory.
  2. Pet House, for the new pets.
  3. Heroes.
  4. Offense buildings.
  5. Defenses: Eagle Artillery → Monolith → merged defenses (Ricochet Cannon, Multi-Archer Tower) → Inferno Towers → Scattershots → X-Bows and Air Defenses (equal priority) → other defenses → traps.
  6. Walls.

Bases: TH16 layouts.

Town Hall 17

The order stays the same, with longer timers. Upgrade the Hero Hall before your heroes, because it raises their maximum level. The Town Hall weapon can be upgraded again (levels 1 to 5): do it before the Monolith.

  1. Laboratory.
  2. Hero Hall, then heroes.
  3. Pet House.
  4. Offense buildings.
  5. Defenses: Town Hall weapon → Eagle Artillery → Monolith → merged defenses (Multi-Gear Tower, Ricochet Cannon, Multi-Archer Tower) → Inferno Towers → Scattershots → X-Bows and Air Defenses (equal priority) → other defenses → traps.
  6. Walls.

Bases: TH17 layouts.

Town Hall 18

The highest level, so there is nothing left to rush toward. On defense, upgrade the Logger first, then the Longshot and the Smasher.

  1. Laboratory.
  2. Hero Hall, then heroes.
  3. Pet House.
  4. Offense buildings.
  5. Defenses: Logger → Longshot and Smasher → Eagle Artillery → Monolith → merged defenses (Multi-Gear Tower, Ricochet Cannon, Multi-Archer Tower) → Inferno Towers → Scattershots → X-Bows and Air Defenses (equal priority) → other defenses → traps.
  6. Walls. Expect to be walling for a long time.

Bases: TH18 layouts.

Common Mistakes to Avoid

  • Upgrading defenses before offense. A strong base still loses loot to a strong army. A strong army wins wars and farms faster.
  • Leaving the Laboratory idle overnight. It is the most common mistake in the game, and the most costly.
  • Spending a builder on walls while a hero waits. Walls get the builders and loot that are left over.
  • Letting storages fill up. Once they are full, the loot from every extra attack is wasted. Spend it on walls: the wall calculator shows how many your storages can pay for.
  • Moving up with outdated research. You can upgrade your Town Hall whenever you like, but a higher Town Hall with a weak army only makes you a bigger target.

Laboratory first. Offense second. Walls with whatever is left.

FAQ

What should I upgrade first in Clash of Clans?

The Laboratory, at every new Town Hall. Then offense (heroes and offense buildings), then defenses, and walls last.

Should I upgrade offense or defense first in Clash of Clans?

Offense. A stronger army wins more wars and farms faster, and that loot pays for your defenses later.

When should I upgrade my Town Hall in Clash of Clans?

Whenever you feel ready. You don't need to max a Town Hall before moving up. The best time is just before Hammer Jam, when upgrades are half price.

Summary
  • At every Town Hall: offense first, then defense, then walls.
  • The real secret: upgrade the Laboratory first at every new Town Hall, and never let it sit idle.
  • Start your longest research and upgrades before you log off.
  • From TH14, the Pet House works like a second Laboratory: no builder needed, so keep it busy.
  • From TH7, keep one builder on a hero at all times.
  • Defense order from TH11: Eagle Artillery → Monolith (plus Spell Towers at TH15) → merged defenses → Inferno Towers → Scattershots → X-Bows and Air Defenses → other defenses → traps. At TH17 the Town Hall weapon comes first; at TH18 the Logger does.
  • Rushing and maxing both work. If you move up, do it just before Hammer Jam.
