What Is the Meteorite Builder Skin?

The Meteorite Builder Skin is the first skin for your Builders, and you can't buy it with gems or money. It costs 3,200 Sparky Stones in the Fancy Shop, and the only way to get Sparky Stones is to finish upgrades.

You buy it once and it covers every Builder in your village. To switch it on, tap any Builder's Hut and pick the Meteorite look. You can switch back to the classic Builder at any time.

How Do You Earn Sparky Stones?

By finishing two kinds of upgrades. Every completed Supercharge level pays 10 stones. Every completed Crafted Defense module level pays 8 stones. Nothing else in the game gives them.

UpgradeStonesWhat it is
Supercharge10 per levelExtra levels for a building that is already maxed for your Town Hall. Most buildings have 2 or 3.
Crafted Defense module8 per levelThe modules of the seasonal defenses from the Crafting Station. Each defense has 3 modules.

The stones arrive when the upgrade finishes, not when you start it. An upgrade that is still running pays nothing yet.

The Fancy Shop is in the Shop from Town Hall 12 to 18. Below Town Hall 12 you can't see it or earn stones. Your balance can hold up to 5,000 stones.

How Many Upgrades Is 3,200 Stones?

A lot more than most players expect. Here is what 3,200 stones means in finished upgrades:

If you only useYou need
Supercharges320 Supercharge levels
Crafted Defense modules400 module levels
Fully upgraded Crafted Defenses (216 stones each)About 15 of them

A fully upgraded Crafted Defense is 27 module upgrades (3 modules, levels 2 to 10), which pays 216 stones. That is under 7% of the skin. In practice you need both sources, every season.

The Real Secret: Temporary Upgrades, Permanent Stones

Supercharges and Crafted Defenses don't last. A building's Supercharge is cleared when an update gives that building a new regular level. Crafted Defenses leave the game when their season ends.

The stones stay. Every level you finish turns into stones that never expire, so temporary upgrades now leave you something permanent. Your goal is not a fully Supercharged base. Your goal is as many finished levels as possible before they disappear.

When the Fancy Shop launched, Supercell also paid out for the Supercharges and Crafted Defense upgrades that had already been removed from the game. That was a one-time payout. If you played before then, open the Fancy Shop and check your balance before you plan anything: you may be closer to the skin than you think.

What Else Is in the Fancy Shop?

Everything in the Fancy Shop is cosmetic, and every item takes stones away from the skin.

ItemPriceHow often
Meteorite Builder Skin3,200Once
Crafted Statue (decoration)500Once
Builder Bust (decoration)500Once
Charge Effect (Supercharge sparkle on a decoration)100As many as you like

A Charge Effect can't be removed once you put it on a decoration. The Crafted Statue and the Builder Bust each cost 500 stones, which is 50 Supercharge levels of progress toward the skin. If you want the skin, buy the skin first. The decorations and the sparkles can wait.

How to Earn Stones Faster

  1. Upgrade Crafted Defense modules every season. They disappear when the season ends, so every module level you don't finish in time is 8 stones lost for good.
  2. Start with the quickest Supercharges. Every Supercharge level pays the same 10 stones, so short ones earn more stones for each day a Builder spends on them.
  3. Use free Builders on Supercharges. When a Builder has nothing permanent left to do, put it on a Supercharge instead of leaving it idle.
  4. Keep permanent upgrades first. Heroes, the Laboratory and new building levels make your base stronger for good. Never delay them for 10 stones.
Pro tip

Don't start a long Crafted Defense upgrade in the last days of a season. If it hasn't finished when the defenses leave, it pays no stones and the resources are gone.

Mistakes That Cost You Stones

  • Spending stones on Charge Effects first. Ten Charge Effects cost 1,000 stones, almost a third of the skin.
  • Skipping Crafted Defenses because they are temporary. The defense leaves, but the stones you earned from it stay.
  • Letting a season end mid-upgrade. Only finished levels pay. Time your last upgrades so they complete before the season ends.
  • Rushing Supercharges over hero or Laboratory upgrades. The skin is cosmetic. Your attack strength is not.

How Long Does It Take?

It depends on your starting balance. Players who had Supercharges and Crafted Defense upgrades before the Fancy Shop launched start well ahead. Players starting from zero face a grind measured in seasons, not weeks.

The pace also depends on Supercell. New Supercharges and new Crafted Defenses add more stones to earn, so no fixed timeline holds for long. Check your balance at the start of every new Crafting season and plan from there.

FAQ

How do you get the Meteorite Builder Skin in Clash of Clans?

Earn 3,200 Sparky Stones by finishing Supercharges and Crafted Defense upgrades, then buy the skin in the Fancy Shop. It covers every Builder, and you switch it on from any Builder's Hut.

How do you get Sparky Stones in Clash of Clans?

Finish a Supercharge level for 10 stones or a Crafted Defense module level for 8 stones. They can't be bought with gems or money.

Do old Supercharges count toward the Meteorite Builder Skin?

Yes. When the Fancy Shop launched, Supercharges and Crafted Defense upgrades already removed from the game were converted into Sparky Stones. Check your balance in the Fancy Shop.
