Your Checklist for B.O.B

Most players get stuck at five builders. The 6th one can't be bought with gems. You earn it on the Builder Base.

The 6th Builder is called B.O.B, short for “Builder’s Other Builder”. This is how you get him:

  1. Upgrade your Builder Base to Builder Hall 9.
  2. Build the B.O.B Control. It costs 100,000 Builder Gold and starts at level 1.
  3. Finish a requirement, then upgrade the B.O.B Control. Each finished requirement unlocks one more level.
  4. At level 5, you can place B.O.B's Hut in your Home Village. That is your 6th builder, and he stays for good.

The upgrades go in this order, but the work doesn't have to. You can work on all four requirements at the same time. Start with the slowest ones, not the easiest.

Should You Max Your Builder Base?

No. Rush it. Only upgrade what the four requirements need. Anything else uses up builders and resources that should go toward B.O.B.

Three Builder Halls matter:

  • Builder Hall 6 gives you O.T.T.O, a second builder for the Builder Base. Get here first.
  • Builder Hall 9 lets you build the B.O.B Control.
  • Builder Hall 10 unlocks the Electro Fire Wizard, the shortcut for Requirement 2. This is the Builder Hall to aim for.

What to upgrade first, at every Builder Hall: heroes → anything a requirement needs → everything else can wait.

Requirement 1: Gear Up the Big Three

A gear up is a special upgrade for three Home Village defenses: the Cannon, the Archer Tower and the Mortar. You need all three.

Requirement 2: Skip to Level 18

This requirement asks for one Builder Base troop at level 18. Most troops start at level 1, so getting one to 18 takes a long chain of Star Lab research.

The shortcut: troops unlocked later don't start at level 1. They start at a minimum level, and the later the troop, the higher it starts.

The Electro Fire Wizard starts at level 17. One upgrade takes it from the day you unlock it to the level B.O.B needs.

  1. At Builder Hall 10, upgrade the Builder Barracks to level 12. The Electro Fire Wizard unlocks at level 17.
  2. Upgrade the Star Lab to level 10. Level 18 research needs it.
  3. Start the level 18 research. Finish it right away with a Book of Fighting or a Hammer of Fighting from the League Shop.

That is the only troop you need to research. You don't even have to attack with it.

Requirement 3: One Mortar, Two Requirements

This requirement asks for any Builder Base defense at level 9. Pick the Multi Mortar.

The Mortar gear up already needs the Multi Mortar at level 8. One more upgrade takes it to 9 and finishes this requirement too. Any other defense has to climb all the way to 9 on its own.

Requirement 4: Balance Your Heroes

Battle MachineBattle Copter

The levels of your Battle Machine and Battle Copter must add up to 45. Take the Battle Machine to 23 and the Battle Copter to 22.

Both heroes cost the same at every level from 16 up: level 23 costs 3.3M Builder Elixir on either one. Each level costs more and takes longer than the one before. So a split that pushes one hero high pays for the most expensive levels, and leaves the other hero's cheap ones unused.

Counted from both heroes at level 15. The Builder Elixir gap is real but small. The time gap is the reason to split evenly: 23/22 finishes in about 5 weeks, 30/15 takes over 10.

  • Put the extra level on the Battle Machine. Its levels 16 to 20 take 4 days each, the Battle Copter's take 5. Same Elixir, 5 days sooner.
  • Battle Machine already past 23? Don't level it further. Upgrade the Battle Copter until the two add up to 45.
  • Never leave a hero idle. Heroes are usually the slowest requirement. The 35 days only hold if both upgrade at the same time, nonstop.

The Order to Follow

Start the Multi Mortar early. It takes the longest of the three to get ready.

Go from top to bottom. Everything except the last step can start before the B.O.B Control is built.

  1. Reach Builder Hall 6 to get O.T.T.O, your second Builder Base builder.
  2. Keep both heroes upgrading nonstop, from the day you unlock them.
  3. Prepare the gear ups. On the Builder Base: Double Cannon to 4, Archer Tower to 6, Multi Mortar to 8. Then do the three gear ups in your Home Village.
  4. Reach Builder Hall 9 and build the B.O.B Control.
  5. Upgrade the Multi Mortar to level 9.
  6. Reach Builder Hall 10. Builder Barracks to 12, Star Lab to 10, then take the Electro Fire Wizard to 18 with a Book or Hammer of Fighting.
  7. Upgrade the B.O.B Control each time a requirement is done. At level 5, place B.O.B's Hut in your Home Village.

The Real Secret: One-Troop Elixir Farming

Builder Elixir is what slows you down. Hero upgrades and Star Lab research use a lot of it. Don't farm it with full attacks. Farm it with one troop.

  1. Start a Builder Base attack.
  2. Drop one troop.
  3. Tap Surrender.

Why it works: every attack you start, even one you surrender, also sends an attack at your own base. Your defenses earn Builder Elixir for defending it, and it lands in the Elixir Cart next to the Boat.

Each run takes a few seconds, so you can do many in a row. The cart has a limit: empty it before it fills up.

Only use it when both are true
  • Only Elixir upgrades are left. Surrendering earns no Builder Gold.
  • You don't mind losing trophies. Every surrender costs trophies. Trophies set your Builder Base league, and your league sets how much the Star Bonus pays. Drop a league and every Star Bonus gets smaller.

Speed-Ups You Are Leaving Behind

  • Raid Medals: every week, buy the Builder Gold and Builder Elixir deals in the Trader's Raid Medals tab first. They pay for the upgrades B.O.B needs. Spend what's left on Clock Tower Potions.
  • Star Bonus: collect it every time it is ready.
  • Clock Tower: upgrade it early and use it. From Builder Hall 6, when both builders are busy, each boost speeds up two upgrades at once.
  • B.O.T.O's Shack (1,000 gems): one more Builder Base builder, so one more upgrade runs at the same time.

Your Daily Routine

  1. Collect your Star Bonus.
  2. Start every upgrade you can afford, heroes first.
  3. Only Elixir upgrades left? Farm with one-troop attacks until you can pay for the next one, then empty the Elixir Cart.
  4. Use the Clock Tower.
  5. Once a week, buy Builder Gold and Builder Elixir from the Trader with Raid Medals.

Don't Wait for Hammer Jam to Unlock B.O.B

Hammer Jam discounts Home Village upgrades, not Builder Base upgrades. Keep working on B.O.B's requirements as soon as you can; waiting for Hammer Jam will not make Builder Base progress cheaper or faster. Once unlocked, B.O.B can help with discounted Home Village upgrades during the event.

Hammer Jam usually returns in early November.

Stop Wasting Months Like This

  • Upgrading Builder Base walls. Walls don't count for any of the four requirements.
  • Upgrading random troops. Only the Electro Fire Wizard needs an upgrade. Every other troop upgrade spends Elixir your heroes need.
  • Leaving a hero idle. Heroes are usually the slowest requirement, so every idle day pushes B.O.B back.
  • Stopping play while heroes upgrade. You will need Builder Elixir for the next level, so keep farming.

How Long Will It Take?

About 3 to 6 months if you play every day. If you skip days, expect about twice as long.

Three things make it faster: one-troop Elixir farming, spending Raid Medals every week, and using a Book of Fighting or Hammer of Fighting on the Electro Fire Wizard.

FAQ

How do you unlock the 6th Builder in Clash of Clans?

Reach Builder Hall 9 and build the B.O.B Control. Then complete four requirements: three gear ups, a troop at level 18, a defense at level 9, and 45 hero levels in total. When the B.O.B Control reaches level 5, B.O.B unlocks.

How long does it take to unlock the 6th Builder?

About 3 to 6 months if you play every day. Playing less can take about twice as long.

What is the fastest way to get the 6th Builder?

Rush the Builder Base, use a Book of Fighting or Hammer of Fighting to take the Electro Fire Wizard to level 18, split the heroes 23 and 22, and farm Builder Elixir with one-troop surrender attacks once only Elixir upgrades are left.
