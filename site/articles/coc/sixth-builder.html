Four Requirements, One Free Builder

Most players get stuck at five builders. The 6th one is free, but you can't buy it with gems. You unlock it on the Builder Base.

The 6th Builder is called B.O.B. This is how you get him:

  1. Upgrade your Builder Base to Builder Hall 9.
  2. Build the B.O.B Control Hut. It costs about 100k Builder Gold and starts at level 1.
  3. Finish a requirement, then upgrade the hut. Every finished requirement lets the hut go up one level.
  4. When the hut reaches level 5, you can place B.O.B's Hut in your Home Village. That is your 6th builder, and you keep him for good.

The hut asks for the requirements in this order. You don't have to wait, though: you can work on all four at the same time. Start with the slowest ones, not the easiest.

Should You Max Your Builder Base?

No. Rush it. Only upgrade what the four requirements need. Anything else uses up builders and resources that should go toward B.O.B.

Three Builder Halls matter:

  • Builder Hall 6 gives you O.T.T.O, a second builder for the Builder Base. Get here first.
  • Builder Hall 9 lets you build the B.O.B Control Hut.
  • Builder Hall 10 unlocks the Electro Fire Wizard, the shortcut for Requirement 2. This is the Builder Hall to aim for.

What to upgrade first, at every Builder Hall: heroes → anything a requirement needs → everything else can wait.

Requirement 1: Gear Up the Big Three

A gear up is a special upgrade for three Home Village defenses: the Cannon, the Archer Tower and the Mortar. You need all three.

Requirement 2: Skip to Level 18

This requirement asks for one Builder Base troop at level 18. Getting a normal troop that high takes a long chain of Star Lab upgrades. There is a shortcut: the Electro Fire Wizard already starts at level 17 when you unlock him, so it needs only one upgrade.

Electro Fire Wizard
  1. At Builder Hall 10, upgrade the Builder Barracks to level 12. This unlocks the Electro Fire Wizard at level 17.
  2. Upgrade the Star Lab to level 10. You need it to research level 18.
  3. Start the Electro Fire Wizard's level 18 research. Finish it right away with a Book of training or a hammer from the League Shop, which you buy with Clan War League medals.

That is the only troop you need to research. You don't even have to attack with it.

Requirement 3: One Mortar, Two Requirements

This requirement asks for any Builder Base defense at level 9. Pick the Multi Mortar.

Here is why: Requirement 1 already makes you upgrade the Multi Mortar to level 8 (the Mortar gear up on the home village needs it). One more upgrade takes it to level 9, and this requirement is done too. Any other defense would have to be upgraded all the way to level 9 on its own.

Requirement 4: Balance Your Heroes

Battle MachineBattle Copter

The levels of your Battle Machine and Battle Copter must add up to 45. Aim for 23 on one and 22 on the other.

  • Keep them even. Every level costs more than the one before, so two mid-level heroes are cheaper than one high-level hero.
  • The Battle Copter starts at level 15. It only needs 7 or 8 more levels. The Battle Machine needs the most upgrades.
  • Never leave a hero idle. Heroes are usually the slowest requirement. Both can upgrade at the same time, so start a new upgrade as soon as one finishes.

The Order to Follow

Follow these steps from top to bottom. You can start the upgrades for each requirement before the hut is even built.

  1. Reach Builder Hall 6 to get O.T.T.O, your second Builder Base builder.
  2. Get the gear ups ready. On the Builder Base, upgrade the Double Cannon to level 4, the Archer Tower to level 6 and the Multi Mortar to level 8. Then do the three gear ups in your Home Village.
  3. Reach Builder Hall 9 and build the B.O.B Control Hut.
  4. Keep both heroes upgrading nonstop, from the day you unlock them.
  5. Reach Builder Hall 10. Upgrade the Builder Barracks to level 12 and the Star Lab to level 10. Unlock the Electro Fire Wizard and use a Hammer to take it to level 18.
  6. Upgrade the Multi Mortar to level 9.
  7. Upgrade the hut each time a requirement is done. At level 5, place B.O.B's Hut in your Home Village.

The Real Secret: One-Troop Elixir Farming

Builder Elixir is what slows you down. Hero upgrades and Star Lab research use a lot of it. Don't farm it with full attacks. Farm it with one troop.

  1. Start a Builder Base attack.
  2. Drop one troop.
  3. Tap Surrender.

Why it works: every attack you start, even one you surrender, also sends an attack at your own base. Your defenses earn Builder Elixir from defending it. How much depends on the defense. The Elixir goes into the Elixir Cart next to the Boat.

Each run takes a few seconds, so you can do many in a row. The cart has a limit, so empty it before it fills up.

When to use it

Once your Builder Gold upgrades are done and only Elixir upgrades are left. Surrendering loses trophies and gives no Builder Gold. Trophies don't matter for B.O.B, so you can ignore them while you farm.

Speed-Ups You Are Leaving Behind

  • Raid Medals: every week, spend them at the Trader on Resources (gold or elixir) or on Clock Tower Potions.
  • Star Bonus: collect it every time it is ready.
  • Clock Tower: upgrade it early and use it. From Builder Hall 6, when both builders are busy, each boost speeds up two upgrades at once.
  • Boto Shack (costs gems): gives you one more Builder Base builder, so more upgrades run at the same time.

Your Daily Routine

  1. Collect your Star Bonus.
  2. Start every upgrade you can afford, heroes first.
  3. Farm Builder Elixir with one-troop attacks until you can pay for your next upgrade. Then empty the Elixir Cart.
  4. Use the Clock Tower.
  5. Once a week, spend your Raid Medals at the Trader.

Save the Slow Upgrades for Hammer Jam

During Hammer Jam, Builder Base upgrades cost half as much and take half the time. The discount only applies to upgrades you start during the event, so hold your longest upgrades until it begins.

The next Hammer Jam is expected in early November 2026.

Stop Wasting Months Like This

  • Upgrading Builder Base walls. Walls don't count for any of the four requirements.
  • Upgrading random troops. Only the Electro Fire Wizard needs an upgrade. Every other troop upgrade spends Elixir your heroes need.
  • Leaving a hero idle. Heroes are usually the slowest requirement, so every idle day pushes B.O.B back.
  • Stopping play while heroes upgrade. You will need Builder Elixir for the next level, so keep farming.

How Long Will It Take?

About 3 to 6 months if you play every day. If you skip days, expect about twice as long.

Three things make it faster: one-troop Elixir farming, spending Raid Medals every week, and using a Hammer on the Electro Fire Wizard.

FAQ

How do you unlock the 6th Builder in Clash of Clans?

Reach Builder Hall 9 and build the B.O.B Control Hut. Then complete four requirements: three gear ups, a troop at level 18, a defense at level 9, and 45 hero levels in total. When the hut reaches level 5, B.O.B unlocks.

How long does it take to unlock the 6th Builder?

About 3 to 6 months if you play every day. Playing less can take about twice as long.

What is the fastest way to get the 6th Builder?

Rush the Builder Base, use a Hammer to take the Electro Fire Wizard to level 18, keep both heroes upgrading until they add up to 45 levels, and farm Builder Elixir with one-troop surrender attacks.

Summary
  • The 6th Builder is B.O.B. Build the B.O.B Control Hut at Builder Hall 9 and upgrade it to level 5.
  • Four requirements: three gear ups, a level 18 troop, a level 9 defense and 45 hero levels.
  • Rush the Builder Base. Builder Hall 10 is the one to aim for.
  • Use a League Shop Hammer on the Electro Fire Wizard to reach level 18 in one upgrade.
  • Upgrade the Multi Mortar to level 9. It covers the Mortar gear up and the defense requirement.
  • Split the heroes 23 and 22. Keeping them even is cheaper.
  • Skip Builder Base walls and random troop upgrades.
  • The secret: once only Elixir upgrades are left, drop one troop and surrender. Your defenses earn Builder Elixir, which you collect from the Elixir Cart.
