Start Here: Every Pet Upgrade Has a Cost

The Pet House upgrades one pet at a time, and every upgrade costs Dark Elixir that your heroes also need. An upgrade spent on the wrong pet makes the pet your attacks need wait longer.

Your goal is not a maxed Pet House. Your goal is stronger attacks.

This guide shows which pets your Town Hall can use, ranks all 12 by long-term value, matches each pet with its best heroes, and gives an upgrade order for Town Hall 14 to 18. It also covers the three pets most players should not upgrade at all.

The Real Secret: Frosty Is Underrated

Most players walk past Frosty in the Pet House. Top players don't. It is one of the strongest pets in the game, and it ranks S tier here, right behind Phoenix and Spirit Fox.

Frosty works with any hero. In a funnel, it makes a single hero more independent, so the hero clears one side of the base and your main army enters cleanly.

Frosty

Frosty unlocks at Town Hall 15. Upgrade it right after Phoenix, and keep leveling it.

Plan the attack it supports in the Army Maker.

When Does Each Pet Unlock?

From Town Hall 14 to 18. Each Town Hall in that range unlocks new pets through the Pet House. You can only upgrade the pets you have, so start with the best pet at your level, not the best pet in the game.

TH15Phoenix, Frosty, Diggy, Poison LizardPhoenix → Frosty → Diggy → Poison Lizard
TH16Spirit Fox, Angry JellySpirit Fox → Angry Jelly

The Town Hall 15 pets replace the Town Hall 14 pets. Unicorn is the only one worth maxing; Dark Elixir spent on the other three is wasted once you move up.

Town Hall 14: Max Unicorn, Skip the Rest

The Pet House opens at Town Hall 14 with four pets. Max Unicorn and leave the other three at the level they unlock at.

UnicornElectro OwlL.A.S.S.IMighty Yak

Unicorn is the only one that earns its Dark Elixir: it helps a hero stay alive, and it stays useful at Town Hall 15. Electro Owl, L.A.S.S.I and Mighty Yak get replaced by the Town Hall 15 pets, so every level you buy them is Dark Elixir your heroes could have used.

Pro tip

Max Unicorn, skip the other three, and move to Town Hall 15. The gap between Town Hall 14 and 15 is the biggest power spike in the game.

If you plan to stay at Town Hall 14 for a long time, you can upgrade a few of the others, but it is not recommended. Moving up to Town Hall 15 makes your attacks far stronger than any Town Hall 14 pet upgrade.

Completion players who max everything before moving up can level the rest in this order: Electro Owl → Mighty Yak → L.A.S.S.I.

Town Hall 15: Phoenix, Then Frosty

Town Hall 15 adds four pets, and this is where your pet upgrades start. Upgrade Phoenix first. It keeps a hero in the fight after a hit that would have taken it out, which helps almost every army.

PhoenixFrostyDiggyPoison Lizard

Frosty comes next, and it is the upgrade most players miss. It works with any hero, and in a funnel it lets that hero clear one side of the base before your main army goes in.

Diggy comes after Frosty, as situational support for a ground hero. Leave Poison Lizard for last.

Town Hall 16: Spirit Fox First

Spirit Fox unlocks here and goes straight to the top of your list. Its invisibility protects a hero through the most dangerous part of the base. With Phoenix from Town Hall 15, you now have both S-tier pets.

Spirit FoxAngry Jelly

Upgrade Angry Jelly next. For about 30 seconds, it makes its hero target only defenses and ignore everything else.

Town Hall 17: Sneezy, With One Catch

Sneezy is still a good pet, but it has one big downside. When its Boogers walk into the Clan Castle's range, they trigger it, and the Clan Castle troops come out early, often straight into your hero.

Sneezy

Sneezy's Boogers also no longer trigger traps, which removes one reason to pair her with a Warden charge. Sneezy still works on a Warden charge at Town Hall 17, but once Greedy Raven unlocks at Town Hall 18, the Raven takes over as the first pick.

Town Hall 18: Greedy Raven for Charges

Greedy Raven is the pet for Warden and Queen charges. It clears trash buildings fast, high-hitpoint storages included, so the charge moves through the edge of the base noticeably faster.

Greedy Raven

On a Warden charge, pick Greedy Raven first and keep Sneezy as the second option. The Raven clears the route without triggering the Clan Castle early.

Pet Upgrade Mistakes to Avoid

  • Upgrading every Town Hall 14 pet. Max Unicorn only. The Town Hall 15 pets replace the other three, so that Dark Elixir belongs to your heroes.
  • Staying at Town Hall 14 too long. The jump to Town Hall 15 is the biggest power spike in the game. Move up instead of leveling Town Hall 14 pets.
  • Treating the tier list as your upgrade order. The tiers assume every pet is unlocked. Start with the best pet your Town Hall has.
  • Maxing every pet by default. The attacks you actually run decide where Dark Elixir goes.
  • Ignoring Frosty. It is S tier, it works with any hero, and most players never upgrade it.
  • Picking Sneezy over Greedy Raven for a Warden charge. Sneezy's Boogers trigger the Clan Castle when they enter its range. The Raven doesn't.
  • Leaving Greedy Raven out of charge armies. It clears trash buildings and storages quickly for Warden and Queen charges.
  • Upgrading Poison Lizard ahead of useful pets. It is the lowest priority of all 12.

FAQ

Which pet should I upgrade first in Clash of Clans?

It depends on your Town Hall. At TH14, max Unicorn and skip the other three, because the TH15 pets replace them. At TH15, upgrade Phoenix and then Frosty. At TH16, add Spirit Fox.

Is Frosty worth upgrading in Clash of Clans?

Yes. Frosty is S tier and one of the most underrated pets in the game. It works with any hero, and in a funnel or distraction it makes a single hero more independent. Upgrade it right after Phoenix.

Should I use Sneezy or Greedy Raven for a Warden charge?

Greedy Raven first, Sneezy second. Sneezy's Boogers trigger the Clan Castle when they walk into its range, pulling its troops toward the Warden. They also no longer trigger traps, which removes one reason to pair her with a Warden charge.
