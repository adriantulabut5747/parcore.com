Start Here: Spend Dark Elixir on Your Army

There are more pets than you need to max. Your Dark Elixir should go to the pets that make your current attacks stronger, not every pet in the Pet House.

This guide ranks all 12 pets, then shows what to prioritize as you move from Town Hall 14 to 18. The overall tier is a starting point; your army decides the final pick.

The Real Secret: Upgrade for Your Attacks

Unlock new options as you advance, then spend Dark Elixir on the pets that improve the attacks you actually use. Do not max every pet by default.

A lower-tier pet can still earn an upgrade when it has a clear job in your army. Frosty is the best example: its funneling support gives a hero more independence.

Build your army around the strategy you plan to use in the Army Maker, then invest in the pets that support it.

Pet Upgrade Tiers: S to C

These tiers rank overall upgrade value from Town Hall 14 through 18. S is the strongest long-term investment. C is where you spend only after your higher-priority pets are covered. Pets within the same tier are grouped, not ranked against each other; use the Town Hall sections for timing.

Town Hall 14: Your First Pets

Town Hall 14 opens the Pet House and your first four pets. Start with Unicorn if your heroes need help staying alive. It remains useful at Town Hall 15, so the upgrade carries forward.

UnicornElectro OwlL.A.S.S.IMighty Yak

Electro Owl makes sense for air armies. L.A.S.S.I and Mighty Yak can wait while you build the pets that see regular use.

Town Hall 15: Choose What Helps Your Army

Town Hall 15 adds four pets. Phoenix is the top upgrade from this group. Frosty deserves more attention than it gets: use it to funnel a hero down one side so the main army can enter cleanly.

PhoenixFrostyDiggyPoison Lizard

Upgrade Phoenix first for broad value. Put Dark Elixir into Frosty when your attacks need an independent funnel. Diggy is situational; leave Poison Lizard for last.

Town Hall 16: Spirit Fox and Angry Jelly

Spirit Fox is the first upgrade to target here. Its invisibility protects a hero through a dangerous section of the base. Angry Jelly is also high value when its targeting fits your attack.

Spirit FoxAngry Jelly

Town Hall 17: Use Sneezy Selectively

Sneezy can still earn an upgrade for the right army, but it no longer belongs on a Warden charge. Its projectiles do not trigger Air Mines now, and they can draw Clan Castle troops toward the Warden.

Keep Sneezy for attacks where that interaction does not hurt the plan. For a Warden charge, save your priority for Greedy Raven when it unlocks.

Sneezy

Town Hall 18: Put Greedy Raven to Work

Greedy Raven is a strong choice for Warden charges and Queen charges. It clears trash buildings quickly, including high-hitpoint storages, so the charge moves through the edge of the base noticeably faster.

When a Warden charge is part of your attack, use Greedy Raven instead of Sneezy. The Raven helps the Warden clear the route without pulling Clan Castle troops into the charge.

Greedy Raven

Pet Upgrade Mistakes to Avoid

  • Maxing every pet by default. Your active attack strategies decide where Dark Elixir belongs.
  • Ignoring Frosty. Its funneling value can make a hero more independent.
  • Using Sneezy on a Warden charge. Its projectiles no longer trigger Air Mines and can lure Clan Castle troops.
  • Leaving Greedy Raven out of charge armies. It clears trash buildings and storages quickly for Warden and Queen charges.
  • Upgrading Poison Lizard ahead of useful pets. It is the lowest priority here.

FAQ

Which pet should I upgrade first in Clash of Clans?

Spirit Fox and Phoenix are the top overall priorities. At Town Hall 14 and 15, Unicorn is a useful early upgrade, while Frosty is valuable when you use it to funnel a hero.

Is Frosty worth upgrading in Clash of Clans?

Yes. Frosty is especially useful for funneling because it helps a hero clear a side of the base with less support from the main army.

Should I use Sneezy or Greedy Raven for a Warden charge?

Use Greedy Raven. Sneezy's projectiles no longer trigger Air Mines and can lure Clan Castle troops toward the Warden.
