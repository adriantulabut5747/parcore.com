Why Your Ore Always Runs Out

You will run out of ore long before you run out of pieces to spend it on.

Maxing all 42 pieces of hero equipment takes more than 1.6 million Shiny Ore, with Glowy and Starry Ore on top. Nobody gets there, and nobody needs to.

Each hero takes two pieces into an attack. Max the right two, and the rest can sit.

The Real Secret: Max Pairs, Not Everything

Don’t try to max every piece. Pick the pair you actually attack with for each hero, max those two, and stop.

Your goal is not a full Blacksmith. Your goal is a maxed pair on every hero you bring to war.

Then learn the meta. Check what the top 200 equip before you spend: if the best players in the world never equip a piece, your Starry Ore doesn’t belong in it either.

Pairs, not pieces

One maxed piece next to a low one wastes both. Level a pair together, so it hits its strength at the same time.

How to Read These Tiers

Ranked overall. The order holds at every Town Hall, as long as you have the piece unlocked.

SThe best this hero has. Max it first.
AStrong picks. Max them if your army uses them.
BGood in the right army, weaker outside it.
CNiche. Only worth leveling for a strategy that needs it.
DThe weakest. Skip it.

A low tier doesn’t rule a piece out. Two of the pairs below use a C tier piece because it fits one strategy perfectly.

Barbarian King Equipment Tier List

Spiky Ball is the reason the King still smashes through a side of the base.

TierEquipmentWhy
SSpiky BallBounces between buildings for heavy damage on every hit. The King’s strongest piece, and it fits every pair he runs.
AGiant GauntletTurns his hits into splash damage and cuts the damage he takes for a long stretch. Built for groups of defenses.
AStick HorseHe gallops over walls right after deployment, faster and with quicker hits. A straight upgrade over the Rage Vial.
BEarthquake BootsOne stomp opens a wide area of walls. Useful for pathing, light on damage.
BSnake BraceletSnakes spawn as he takes damage. Strong next to the Spiky Ball, but it needs high levels to carry its weight.
CRage VialA short boost to damage and speed. The Stick Horse does the same job better, but it still works with the Gauntlet.
DBarbarian PuppetRaged Barbarians that don’t pull defenses off him. The weakest thing he can carry.
DVampstacheHeals him on every hit, far too slowly to matter against high-level defenses.

Pairs Worth Maxing

Spiky Ball+Stick Horse

For Super Minion attacks with the Warden’s Fireball. Strong with the Angry Jelly too.

Spiky Ball+Snake Bracelet

For funneling with the King: Bowler and Electro Titan attacks, and Queen Charges.

Giant Gauntlet+Rage Vial

Gets the King into the core. Good with the Angry Jelly, and in Rocket Balloon attacks with the Fireball.

Action Figure+Giant Arrow

Takes out Air Defenses and Air Sweepers. Add the Dragon Duke’s Rocket Backpack to bring down high-hitpoint defenses too.

Action Figure+Magic Mirror

Gets the Queen into the core. Walk her in, rage her up, then use the ability.

Action Figure+Monolith Arrow

Built for Queen Charges.

Grand Warden Equipment Tier List

The Warden has two S tiers that do opposite jobs: one deals the damage, the other keeps your army alive.

TierEquipmentWhy
SEternal TomeMakes the Warden and every nearby troop immune to damage. The answer to the core and the Town Hall’s blast. A Common, so it is cheap to max.
SFireballSplash damage on the closest defense, deadly when defenses sit together. His biggest damage piece.
AHeroic TorchNearby troops walk over walls, move faster and take less damage. Built for Bowlers, Throwers and Electro Titans.
BRage GemA passive damage boost for every troop near him, all attack long, and it boosts Healers too. Fireball’s usual partner.
BHealing TomeHeals the Warden and nearby troops back up. Best with Dragons and Dragon Riders.
CLife GemExtra hitpoints for nearby troops. Its one home is Thrower attacks, next to the Rage Gem.
CLavaloon PuppetLavaloons that soak air traps for Dragon armies. Air only, and outclassed there too.

Pairs Worth Maxing

Fireball+Rage Gem

Clears the key defenses before the main army arrives: Clan Castle troops, the Monolith, the Revenge Tower. By the time your troops reach the core, its defenses are gone.

Eternal Tome+Heroic Torch

Gets ground troops into the base: Bowlers, Throwers or Electro Titans. Use the Torch when they are about to get stuck on a wall, and they jump it and head for the core.

Eternal Tome+Healing Tome

For Dragons and Dragon Riders. The Healing Tome tops them back up, so they keep going.

Rage Gem+Life Gem

Thrower attacks only. The Rage Gem boosts the Healers, the Life Gem gives the Throwers extra hitpoints, and they become almost impossible to kill. The only real threat left is the timer.

Rocket Spear+Seeking Shield

Destroys the defenses at the back of the base, the backend. Also the perfect start for a Dragon Rider attack, to set up a clean funnel.

Electro Boots+Frost Flake

Works next to a Barbarian King on Stick Horse and Giant Gauntlet with the Angry Jelly. The King tanks, and when he gets in trouble, the Frost Flake freezes the defenses on him. Also good for Royal Charges.

With Dragon Riders or Hydras. The Meteor Staff snipes the defenses coming up next or close by.

For LaLo or regular Dragons. Low-hitpoint troops fall fast, so the Crown reaches its power spike sooner.

Henchmen Puppet+Dark Crown

With Dragons. When a Monolith or a single Inferno locks on, the Henchmen give him a second life and pull the defense off him.

Where Your Ore Goes First

Commons and Epics cost very different amounts, and that decides the order.

  • Commons max out at level 18 and never cost Starry Ore.
  • Epics go to level 27, cost about twice as much Shiny and Glowy Ore, and they are the only pieces that use Starry Ore.

So spend in this order:

  1. S tier Commons first. Cheap to max and used in almost every attack.
  2. S tier Epics next. This is where your Starry Ore goes.
  3. The second piece of each pair you actually run.
  4. Everything else only when a new strategy needs it.

Stop Wasting Ore Like This

  • Leveling every piece a little. You end up with six half-built pieces and no finished pair.
  • Pouring ore into a new Epic on day one. Wait until it shows up in the meta.
  • Spending Starry Ore on a B or C tier Epic you only try now and then: Snake Bracelet, Frozen Arrow, Dark Crown, Revenge Deck or the Lavaloon Puppet.
  • Skipping the Commons. Four of the eight S tiers are Commons, and none of them cost Starry Ore.
  • Copying a pair without its army. The Heroic Torch shines next to Bowlers, Throwers and Electro Titans, and does nothing for an air army.

FAQ

What is the best hero equipment in Clash of Clans?

Each hero has its own: Spiky Ball for the King, Giant Arrow and Action Figure for the Queen, Fireball and Eternal Tome for the Warden, Rocket Spear for the Royal Champion, Dark Orb for the Minion Prince and Fire Heart for the Dragon Duke.

Should you max all hero equipment in Clash of Clans?

No. Max the pair you attack with on each hero. Maxing all 42 pieces takes more than 1.6 million Shiny Ore.

What should you spend Starry Ore on in Clash of Clans?

Only on Epics you will use for months, starting with the S tier ones: Spiky Ball, Action Figure, Fireball and Rocket Spear. Commons never cost Starry Ore.
