Ask Klee.

KleeKlee knows lots of answers! And tell Klee what to build next.

Where does this page get its numbers?

Wishes, events, codes and resets come from ennead.cc and Project Amber (yatta.moe). Parchrome refreshes them every three hours, so a brand-new code can take a little while to show up.

When do dailies and domains reset?

At 4:00 server time, and each server keeps its own clock: Asia, America and Europe. Pick yours in the Daily Commissions panel at the top and every timer on the page follows it.

Why doesn’t my UID show any characters?

The search reads your in-game profile through Enka.Network. Characters only show if Show Character Details is on in your Character Showcase. Switch it on in game, wait a few minutes, then search again.

Do these codes still work?

They’re the codes still listed as live, but HoYoverse can end one without warning. Redeem them early, and check Redeem codes for the full list.

How does pity work on the character wish?

A 5★ is guaranteed by pull 90, and from about pull 74 the odds climb fast (soft pity). If your 5★ isn’t the featured one, your next 5★ is. Your pity carries over between character wishes.

Who writes the build guides?

Parchrome does, and every build is checked before it goes up. Pick any character in Character Build Guides to open theirs.

Is Parchrome official?

No. Parchrome is a fan site and isn’t affiliated with HoYoverse. Game art and names belong to HoYoverse.
