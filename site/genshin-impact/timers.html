      Did you know

      • A full bar wastes 7.5 resin an hour

        Regeneration stops at 200. An 8-hour night at cap costs 60 resin, three domain runs.

      • Transient Resin expires

        It disappears a week after the Monday following the day you got it. Use it before your Fragile Resin.

      • Over 200 stops the clock

        Refills can push you past 200, but resin won't regenerate again until you're back under it.

      Ask Klee.

      KleeDon’t see your question? Send it to Klee with the form.

      Do I have to keep this tab open?

      No. Your resin and timers are saved on this device and keep counting while it’s closed. For a reminder that pops up anyway, add a timer to your calendar.

      Why does everything reset at 04:00?

      That’s when the game day starts on your server, not midnight where you live, so Asia, America and Europe reset at different hours.

      What happens to resin over 200?

      Refills can push you past 200, but resin won’t regenerate again until you’re back under it.

      Which resin should I use first?

      Transient Resin: it disappears a week after the Monday following the day you got it. Use it before your Fragile Resin.
