    Ask Klee.

    KleeKlee counted every banner since 1.0 for this! Tell Klee what else you want to know.

    How is the wait counted?

    Days since the character's last banner ended. × usual compares that with their average gap between banners, so 2× means they've waited twice as long as they normally do.

    Do Chronicled Wishes count?

    For a 5★, yes: you can aim for them there. For a 4★, no: a Chronicled Wish's 4★s are its pool, not a rate-up, so only Event Wishes count.

    Can you tell who's coming back next?

    No. HoYoverse only says who's next when the next version is announced, and a long wait doesn't promise a rerun. This page shows who's overdue, not who's confirmed.

    Where does the history come from?

    paimon.moe's list of every banner since 1.0, plus Parchrome's live feed, which adds new banners within three hours. Weapon banners aren't tracked yet.
