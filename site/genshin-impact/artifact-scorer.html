    How rolls work.

    Every 5★ artifact follows the same rules. Knowing them is how you tell a keeper from fodder before you spend the EXP.

    1. 1It drops with 3 or 4 substats

      From a domain, 4 only 20% of the time (34% from bosses and strongboxes). A 3-substat piece gets its 4th at +4, which uses up that upgrade.

    2. 2Five upgrades: +4, +8, +12, +16, +20

      Each one picks one of the four substats at random, 25% each. Nothing steers it: crit is as likely as flat DEF.

    3. 3Every roll is one of four sizes

      70%, 80%, 90% or 100% of the biggest roll, 25% each. That's how the scorer counts rolls from a value: 10.5% CRIT Rate can only be three rolls.

    Crit value = 2 × CRIT Rate + CRIT DMG, from the substats. Rate counts twice because a CRIT Rate roll is half the size of a CRIT DMG roll.

    Roll value = each substat you want, as a share of its biggest roll, added up. 100% = one perfect roll; a +20 piece has had 8 or 9 rolls in all.

    Did you know

    • Level to +4 before you judge a 3-liner

      A 3-substat piece shows its 4th at +4. Cheap to check, and the 4th decides most pieces.

    • Reshape a near-miss

      Dust of Enlightenment re-rolls where a piece's upgrades went, with at least 2 of them landing in the two substats you pick (more with Hallowed Exegesis).

    • Define the piece you need

      The Artifact Transmuter makes a piece of your set, main stat and two chosen substats, paid in Sanctifying Elixir from old artifacts.

    Questions, answered.

    About the scores and the maths behind them.

    Is a high crit value always good?

    No. It only counts CRIT Rate and CRIT DMG. A support who needs Energy Recharge, or an EM reaction character, can want a piece with no crit at all. Pick the stats you want and read the roll value instead.

    Why does a substat say "3 or 4 rolls"?

    Some values can be made by two different roll counts (three big rolls or four small ones). The scorer checks which counts add up for the piece's level and shows the likely one; when two are close, it shows both.

    My value says it doesn't match any roll.

    Check for a typo, and that it's a 5★ piece: 4★ artifacts roll smaller numbers and max at +16. The game rounds % stats to one decimal, so type exactly what it shows.

    Can I score a whole build at once?

    Look yourself up in the Player lookup: it shows every artifact on your showcased characters with its crit value and roll count.
