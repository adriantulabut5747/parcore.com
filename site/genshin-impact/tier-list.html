How it works

  • Your list lives in the link

    Every move updates the page's address. Copy it and anyone who opens it sees your exact board, tier names included.

  • Rename and add tiers

    Tap a letter to rename it (up to 20 letters, like "Abyss MVP"). Add tiers down to I, or remove any with its ×.

  • Keyboard works too

    Tab to a tile, press Enter, then 1 to 9 to drop it into that tier, or 0 to send it back to Unranked.
