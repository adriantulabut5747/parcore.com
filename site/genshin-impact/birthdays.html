    How birthdays work.

    KleeWhat happens in the game on someone’s birthday. Klee loves the gifts!

    • They write to you

      On a character's birthday, a letter from them arrives in your in-game mail, with gifts attached.

    • Gifts come with the letter

      Often their special dish or something they made, plus a few materials. The letter in the spotlight lists this year's real attachments.

    • The day starts at the daily reset

      Birthdays follow the server's 04:00 reset, like commissions, so Asia, America and Europe flip at different hours.

    • Claim the gifts in time

      Mail expires. Star a letter to keep it in your Collected Mail after the gifts are claimed.

    • Set your own in your profile

      Pick your birthday in the Paimon Menu profile and you get a letter of your own on the day.

    • A new drawing every year

      HoYoverse posts official birthday art for the character each year on HoYoLAB and the game's socials.

    Ask Klee.

    KleeKlee never forgets a birthday (Jean reminds her)! Tell Klee what else you want to know.

    Why isn’t the Traveler on the calendar?

    The Traveler’s birthday is the one you set in your profile, so it’s different for every player.

    Why doesn’t “today” match my clock?

    Birthdays follow the server’s 04:00 reset, like commissions. Pick your server and the page follows its day.

    Which year’s letter is shown?

    Each character’s newest birthday letter. Older years are on the Genshin Impact Wiki.

    What are the red dots?

    Your characters: the ones you picked on Daily domains. Their birthdays get a red dot, and the calendar file can hold only theirs.
