    How enemies work.

    KleeKlee’s notes from fighting everything. Read these before you blow anything up!

    • Almost everything starts at 10%.

      Unless an enemy says otherwise, it takes 10% less damage from every element and from Physical. That's the dashed line on each chart.

    • Immune means nothing gets through.

      A Pyro Slime takes no Pyro damage at all. Bring another element, or one that puts the fire out.

    • Shields and phases swap the numbers.

      Many enemies change resistance while shielded, stunned or in a new phase. The dossier has a tab for each state the wiki lists.

    • Resistance can drop below zero.

      Shred from sets like Viridescent Venerer or from Superconduct lowers it. Below 0%, each extra point only counts for half.

    • DEF climbs with level.

      Every enemy has 500 DEF plus 5 for each level. HP and ATK grow on their own curves; slide the level in the dossier to see them.

    • Energy drops at HP marks.

      Elites and bosses drop orbs and particles as their HP falls past set marks, not only when they die. Orbs give three times a particle.

    The characters you picked on Daily domains. Their materials come first in the hunt list.

    Many enemies change resistance while shielded, stunned or in a new phase. There’s a tab for each state the wiki lists.
