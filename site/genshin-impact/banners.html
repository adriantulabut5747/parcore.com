    Ask Klee.

    KleeKlee asked around for all of these! Tell Klee what else you want to know.

    Do the two character wishes share pity?

    Yes. Both character wishes count toward the same pity and the same guarantee, and both carry over to the next character wishes. The weapon wish has its own pity, which carries over too.

    What happens if I lose the 50/50?

    Your next 5★ on a character wish is the featured one, so 180 wishes is the most it can take. Since version 5.0, Capturing Radiance can also turn a lost 50/50 into the featured 5★, which lifts the odds to 55%.

    How does Epitomized Path work?

    Chart a course to one of the two featured 5★ weapons. Any other 5★ weapon gives you 1 Fate Point, and with 1 point the next 5★ is your pick. Fate Points reset when the wish ends, so only chart a course you'll finish.

    When is my favourite coming back?

    HoYoverse only says who's next when the next version is announced. Until then, the Rerun tracker shows how long every character has waited since their last banner.
