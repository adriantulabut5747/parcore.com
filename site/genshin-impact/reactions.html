    Lunar reactions.

    KleeLunar reactions are Hydro reactions with a twist! Step 1: who you need. Step 2: how the damage works. Step 3: how to make it stronger. Step 4: the rules.

    Step 1 · Who you need

    Put one of these characters in your team

    While one of them is in your team of four, Hydro's reactions turn into Lunar ones for everyone in the team. Columbina changes all three; the others change one each (the cards at the bottom show which). In the game they carry a Moon Wheel instead of a Vision.

    The characters under Only count for Moonsign don't change any reaction themselves, but they help in step 3.

    Change the reactions
    Only count for Moonsign

    Step 2 · How the damage works

    Your whole team adds to the hit

    A normal reaction uses the stats of whoever set it off. A Lunar one doesn't: the game works out a hit for every character who put Hydro or Electro on the enemy, each with their own stats, then adds them up like this:

    60% of the best + 30% of the 2nd + 5% + 5%

    What it means for you: give your support Elemental Mastery and crit too. With two characters, the second one's hit is about a third of the total. Lunar-Crystallize and Stellar Swirl add up the same way.

      Step 3 · Make it stronger

      More Nod-Krai characters, stronger team

      Every Nod-Krai character in your team (and Zibai) adds 1 to your team's Moonsign level. You can see it on the Team Bonus screen, next to Elemental Resonance.

      1. 1
        Nascent Gleam

        Some of these characters' talent effects only work from this level.

      2. 2+
        Ascendant Gleam

        Those talent effects get stronger, and your other characters start boosting Lunar damage (the box on the right).

      With 2+ Moonsign: a bonus from everyone else

      When a character who is not from Nod-Krai uses their Elemental Skill or Burst, your team's Lunar damage goes up for 20 seconds. How much depends on their element and one of their stats (below), up to 36%. Two of these bonuses don't add together.

      • 0.9% per 100 ATKMax at 4,000 ATK
      • 0.6% per 1,000 HPMax at 60,000 HP
      • 1% per 100 DEFMax at 3,600 DEF
      • 2.25% per 100 EMMax at 1,600 EM

      KleeStep 4: four rules every Lunar hit follows. Klee's tip: build Elemental Mastery and crit, not damage % goblets!

      • It can crit

        It uses your character's Crit Rate and Crit DMG. Normal Bloom or Overloaded never crit.

      • Enemy DEF doesn't matter

        Only the enemy's resistance does, so anything that lowers resistance (like Viridescent Venerer) still helps.

      • Damage % bonuses do nothing

        Elemental DMG goblets and buffs like Furina's or Yelan's don't help. Elemental Mastery and Lunar bonuses do.

      • It needs Hydro

        All three are Hydro reactions, so a team with no Hydro gets no Lunar reactions at all.

      KleeThe three Lunar reactions! “Hit strength 3×” means it hits three times as hard as a 1× reaction, and it grows with your level.

      Stellar Glimmer.

      KleeStellar is like Lunar, but for Cryo! Put the right character in your team and two of Cryo's reactions change.

      Who you need

      Put one of these characters in your team

      While one of them is in your team of four, Cryo's reactions turn into Stellar ones for everyone in the team. The Cryo Traveler does it too; Vesna only changes Swirl. In the game they carry a Stellar Linchpin, a gem that works like a Vision.

      Change the reactions

      Stellar-Conduct · The field

      Hit enemies inside the field

      Stellar-Conduct (Electro + Cryo) leaves a field on the ground. Every 4 seconds it counts your Cryo and Electro hits inside it (up to 12) and gets stronger for the next 4 seconds. Drag the slider to see how much.

      +20%Cryo & Electro DMG
      1.00×Stellar-Conduct damage
      −40%Enemy Physical RES

      Stellar Swirl · The Vortex

      Swirl three times for a bigger blast

      1. 1st Stellar Swirl

        A hit of Anemo damage, plus a small Vortex (Lv. 1) that explodes into Cryo around it a moment later.

      2. 3rd Swirl

        The Vortex grows to Lv. 2: a bigger blast that hits harder (3× instead of 2×).

      3. 3 more Swirls

        It explodes right away, and your next jump within 5 seconds goes higher.

          Did you know?

          KleeKlee found these out by blowing lots of things up. For science!

          • Aino, Jahoda and Illuga don't change any reaction

            They count for Moonsign (step 3), but only the characters in step 1 change reactions.

          • Verdant Dew never expires

            Up to 3, kept through team swaps, teleports, the daily reset and closing the game.

          • Moondrifts count as Geo constructs

            With two Geo characters, Geo Resonance's +15% DMG and Geo resistance cut work from them, even without a shield.

          • Only the biggest hit's crit counts

            An added-up Lunar hit counts as a crit (for Favonius weapons and the like) only if the biggest hit in it crit.

          • One thundercloud at a time

            Trigger Lunar-Charged far from the cloud and a new one forms there; the old one vanishes.

          • Lunar-Bloom's cores are normal Bloom

            The Dendro Cores it leaves deal normal Bloom damage. Only characters' attacks deal Lunar-Bloom damage.

          Ask Klee.

          KleeKlee has tried every reaction (some by accident)! Tell Klee what else you want to know.

          Why do I still get Electro-Charged with Ineffa?

          Ineffa (or Flins, or Columbina) has to be in your active team of four. The change only works while they’re in it, not just because you own them.

          Is Lunar-Charged just a stronger Electro-Charged?

          Not exactly. It strikes every 2 seconds instead of every second, but each strike hits harder (3× against 2×), can crit, and adds up the hits of everyone who put Hydro or Electro on the enemy. Damage % bonuses don’t help it, so build Elemental Mastery and crit.

          Does Furina boost Lunar damage?

          No. Her buff is a damage % bonus, and Lunar damage ignores those. She still puts Hydro on enemies, her own attacks still get the buff, and her stats count if she’s one of the characters whose hits are added up.

          What if I only have one Nod-Krai character?

          One gives Nascent Gleam, which some of their own talent effects need. Two or more give Ascendant Gleam: stronger talent effects, plus a Lunar damage bonus whenever your other characters use their Skill or Burst.

          Can a team have Lunar and Stellar reactions?

          Yes. They change different pairs (Lunar changes Hydro’s, Stellar changes Cryo’s), so a team with both kinds of characters gets both. Hydro and Cryo together still just Freeze.

          Why does Lunar-Bloom show no damage?

          Because the reaction itself deals none. It makes Dendro Cores (normal Bloom damage) and Verdant Dew. Lunar-Bloom damage only comes from some characters’ attacks, like Lauma’s and Nefer’s, and many of them spend the Dew.
