    Lunar reactions.

    Nod-Krai's three Hydro reactions, from Version 5.8 on. Everything that makes them different, step by step.

    Step 1 · The switch

    A Moon Wheel in the party

    Some of Nod-Krai's fighters (and Zibai, from Liyue) carry a Moon Wheel instead of a Vision, running on kuuvahki, an older, purer elemental power. Their passive (Moonsign Benediction) swaps Hydro's reactions for Lunar ones for the whole party. Columbina swaps all three; everyone else swaps one.

    Turns Lunar on
    Raises Moonsign only

    Step 2 · The damage

    Everyone who touched it pays in

    A Lunar-Charged strike isn't the trigger's hit. The game works out a separate hit for every character who put Hydro or Electro on the enemy, each with their own level, Elemental Mastery and crit, then blends them:

    60% of the best + 30% of the 2nd + 5% + 5%

    So build the support's EM and crit too: their hit is a third of the damage. Lunar-Crystallize and Stellar Swirl blend the same way.

      Step 3 · Moonsign

      Count your Moonsign characters

      Every Nod-Krai character (and Zibai) raises the party's Moonsign level by one. It shows on the Team Bonus screen, next to Elemental Resonance.

      1. 1
        Nascent Gleam

        Everyone gets the Nascent Gleam effect, which these characters' talents read.

      2. 2+
        Ascendant Gleam

        Moonsign characters' talents get stronger, and your other characters start buffing Lunar damage (right).

      Ascendant Gleam: the non-Moonsign buff

      For 20 s after a character without Moonsign uses their Skill or Burst, the party's Lunar Reaction DMG goes up by their stat. Up to 36%, and it doesn't stack.

      • 0.9% per 100 ATKMax at 4,000 ATK
      • 0.6% per 1,000 HPMax at 60,000 HP
      • 1% per 100 DEFMax at 3,600 DEF
      • 2.25% per 100 EMMax at 1,600 EM
      • Can crit

        With the character's normal Crit Rate and Crit DMG. Classic Bloom or Overloaded can't.

      • Ignores enemy DEF

        Like the classic transformative reactions. Enemy RES still counts, so shred helps.

      • Ignores DMG Bonus

        Elemental DMG goblets and % DMG buffs (Furina's, Yelan's) do nothing here. EM and Lunar bonuses do.

      • Needs Hydro

        All three are Hydro reactions. A Lunar team with no Hydro just gets classic reactions.

          Did you know

          • Aino, Jahoda and Illuga don't swap anything

            They count toward Moonsign, but only Moon Wheel users and Columbina turn Lunar reactions on.

          • Verdant Dew never expires

            Up to 3, kept through team swaps, teleports, the daily reset and closing the game.

          • Moondrifts count as Geo constructs

            Two Geo characters' resonance gets its +15% DMG and Geo shred from them, shield or no shield.

          • The best contributor decides the crit

            A blended Lunar hit counts as a crit (for Favonius and the like) only if the best character's own hit crit.

          • One thundercloud at a time

            Trigger Lunar-Charged far from the cloud and a new one forms there; the old one vanishes.

          • Lunar-Bloom's cores are plain Bloom

            The Dendro Cores it leaves deal normal Bloom damage. Only talents deal Lunar-Bloom damage.

          Questions, answered.

          What players ask most about the new reactions.

          Why am I still getting Electro-Charged with Ineffa?

          Check that Ineffa (or Flins, or Columbina) is actually in the active party of four: the swap only works while they're in it.

          Is Lunar-Charged just a stronger Electro-Charged?

          No. It hits every 2 seconds instead of every second, but each strike is bigger (3× level against 2×), can crit, and blends the stats of everyone who applied Hydro or Electro. It also ignores % DMG Bonus, so build EM and crit instead.

          Does Furina buff Lunar damage?

          No. Her buff is DMG Bonus, which Lunar damage ignores. She still applies Hydro, her own attacks still get the buff, and her stats count if she's one of the characters blended into the hit.

          What does Moonsign do if I only have one Nod-Krai character?

          One gives Nascent Gleam, which their own talents need. Two or more give Ascendant Gleam: stronger Moonsign talents, plus a Lunar DMG buff from your other characters' Skills and Bursts.

          Can a team have both Lunar and Stellar reactions?

          Yes. They swap different pairs (Lunar = Hydro, Stellar = Cryo), so a party with both kinds of characters gets both swaps. Hydro and Cryo together still just Freeze.

          Why does Lunar-Bloom show no damage when it triggers?

          Because the reaction deals none. It makes Dendro Cores (normal Bloom damage) and Verdant Dew. Lunar-Bloom damage only comes from talents, like Lauma's and Nefer's, many of which spend the Dew.
