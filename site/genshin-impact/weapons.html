    Weapon details

    Ask Klee.

    KleeKlee only needs a catalyst, but she checked every weapon anyway! Tell Klee what else you want to know.

    What do the day filters mean?

    The days that weapon’s ascension material domain is open. Every domain is open on Sunday, and the day marked red is today on your server.

    Do the stats include refinement?

    No. They’re the game’s base values at each level, before refinement or artifacts. Open a weapon to see them level by level.

    What’s the “On the weapon wish now” box?

    The weapons on the current Epitome Invocation and when it ends. It only shows while a weapon wish is running; Banner details has the rates.

    When do new weapons show up?

    The list is checked for new weapons every three hours from Project Amber, so a new weapon joins within hours of its release.

    Current banners

    The two 5-star weapons on the Epitome Invocation right now, and when it ends.
    All characters

    Every playable character, by nation or newest first.
