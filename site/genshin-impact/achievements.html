    How achievements work.

    The rules behind the checklist.

    • Stars are tiers

      An achievement with two or three stars is two or three achievements in one. Each star pays its own Primogems: usually 5, then 10, then 20.

    • Claim the Primogems yourself

      Finishing one isn't enough. Open Paimon Menu, then Achievements, and claim the reward, or the Primogems wait there.

    • A full category pays a namecard

      Finish every achievement in a category and its namecard is yours to wear on your profile. 71 categories give one.

    • Two never finish

      Wonders of the World and Memories of the Heart grow with every update, so they give no namecard and never reach 100%.

    • Some are hidden

      Hidden achievements don't show in the menu until you've earned them, so the game's list can look shorter than this one.

    • Quests lead to many

      Hundreds come from a quest. The checklist names that quest under the achievement ("Part of").
