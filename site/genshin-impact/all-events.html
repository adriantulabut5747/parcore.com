    Ask Klee.

    KleeKlee keeps a calendar of all of these! Tell Klee what else you want to know.

    When do the Abyss and the Theater reset?

    The Spiral Abyss resets on the 16th of every month and the Imaginarium Theater on the 1st, both at 04:00 server time. Each section above counts down to its own reset.

    How many Primogems do they give?

    Up to 800 a month from the Spiral Abyss and 1,000 from the Imaginarium Theater, so 1,800 for clearing both.

    Why does everything end later on my server?

    Wishes and events end at the same clock time on every server, so America's end 13 hours after Asia's and Europe's 7. A new version is different: it starts at the same moment worldwide.

    Why isn't the next version here?

    HoYoverse doesn't list events before they're announced, so this page only shows what's running this version. New ones show up within three hours of going live.
