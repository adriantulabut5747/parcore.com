      Is this the game’s real map?

      No. It’s a drawing: each region sits where its official description puts it.

      Why are some characters in no region?

      Characters are counted by the nation the game lists them under. The Traveler, Aloy, Skirk and Nicole aren’t tied to one.

      Why doesn’t “Open today” match my clock?

      It follows the server you pick. The game day starts at 04:00 server time, so Asia, America and Europe change at different hours.
