    Did you know

    • A slow first half steals your stars

      The second half only gets the time the first half leaves behind.

    • Missed a star? Restart before the last kill

      Once a chamber is cleared, redoing it means replaying the floor from Chamber 1.

    • Unclaimed Primogems vanish on the 16th

      Nothing is mailed after the reset. Claim your star rewards before it.

    • Hate today's buffs? Wait a day

      The Benediction choices in every chamber reroll at the daily reset.

    • Floor 12 enemies have 2.5× HP

      Floors 8 to 11 already double their open-world HP.

    • Build for floors 9 and 10 once a year

      They usually change only at each X.0 update. Floors 11 and 12 change every version.

    Imaginarium Theater.

    This month's season.

    Loading the season…

    Did you know

    • Extra characters are free stats

      Each one over the minimum adds 2 Blessing Levels. Every level is +800 HP, +50 ATK, +50 DEF and +20 EM.

    • Owning an opener buffs you everywhere

      Yours get +20% HP, ATK and DEF all season, in the open world too.

    • Every character only gets two acts

      Each act costs all four members 1 of their 2 Vigor. Put someone you won't need in the last slot.

    • Play guest stars early

      They skip the element rule, so they miss the season's reaction blessings.

    • Visionary stops at 800 Primogems

      The last 200 of the 1,000 sit in Lunar Mode's two Arcanum challenges.

    • Every act starts with full Energy

      Open each fight with your Bursts.
