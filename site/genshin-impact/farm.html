    How domains work.

    The rules behind the schedule.

    • Sunday opens every domain

      Every talent book and weapon material, from every nation, can be farmed on Sundays.

    • The schedule runs twice a week

      Thursday repeats Monday, Friday repeats Tuesday and Saturday repeats Wednesday. Miss a day and it's back in three.

    • Domains rotate at 04:00 server time

      The same daily reset as commissions, so Asia, America and Europe flip at different hours. Pick your server above.

    • One run costs 20 Original Resin

      A Condensed Resin claims two runs' rewards at once, which saves time, not resin.

    • Three of a tier craft one of the next

      3 Teachings make a Guide and 3 Guides make a Philosophies at an Alchemy Bench, for Mora. Weapon materials work the same way.

    • Purple books need a higher domain level

      Philosophies and the gold weapon materials only drop from a domain's top levels, which unlock with Adventure Rank.
