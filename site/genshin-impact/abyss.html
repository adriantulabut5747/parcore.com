    Ask Klee.

    KleeThe Abyss is scary, but Klee knows the rules! Tell Klee what else you want to know.

    When does the Abyss reset?

    On the 16th of every month at 04:00 server time. Claim your star rewards before then: nothing is mailed after the reset.

    How many Primogems does it give?

    Up to 800 each period, for every star on floors 9 to 12.

    Why did I run out of time in the second half?

    Both halves share the chamber's timer, so the second half only gets the time the first half leaves behind. Clear the first half fast.

    Do floors 9 and 10 change too?

    Usually only at each X.0 update, so a team for them lasts about a year. Floors 11 and 12 change every version.
