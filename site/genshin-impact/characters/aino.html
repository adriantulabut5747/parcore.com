Her story

A genius mechanic and inventor from Nod-Krai who loves interesting machines and sweet treats.

Located at the border, Nod-Krai is neither a land of abundance nor a place of joy. All year round, the cold wind howls and lashes the lands, as though attempting to wring even the last dregs of warmth from every corner.

Read more

Yet in this seemingly forgotten corner of the world, the marketplace is chock-a-block with an astonishing array of marvelous machines. From guns that shoot flowers to floor sweepers that act as affectionate as puppies... You never know how a contraption might act until the very moment it springs to life. These inventions have brought comfort and laughter to this desolate land, and their creator is none other than Aino, the owner of the Clink-Clank Krumkake Craftshop.

Aino has spent years secluded in this unique workshop with not a single plushie (something children should love) in sight. Instead, she surrounds herself with sheets upon sheets of cold metal and mechanical parts piled precariously high. But this doesn't stop Aino from seeing it as the bestest and warmest home in the world. Here, she enjoys the companionship of numerous family members — assembled by her from screws and steel.

Aino, who calls herself their "parent," takes on the responsibility of caring for her little mechanical family every day. In the morning, they need to "wake up" by powering on, they have to "charge" themselves up with lunch at noon, then "sleep" in the night by powering down, right on schedule. Whatever spare time they have, they spend on diligently "executing tasks."

Besides the creations she makes as a hobby, this genius mechanic also takes on various mechanical commissions. From the massive Boom and Blast Kuuvahki Energy Cannon that defends everyone from the Wild Hunt's invasions, to tiny precision parts in mechanical arms for aged adventurers... As long as you sincerely praise this genius mechanic's brilliant ideas and offer her delicious desserts, she'll create masterpieces beyond your wildest imagination.

However, her inventions occasionally come with bizarre features not included in the original commission's requirements. In that sense, placing an order with Aino is like buying a box of chocolates... except that the box has a chance to fire a scary doll at you when you open it — so you'd best pray it doesn't!

Aino drops her Cool Your Jets Ducky with her Burst, then swaps out while it fires water balls at nearby enemies. Her job is steady Hydro, and just being in the party raises the Moonsign by one level. Her own damage scales from EM and mostly comes through reactions, so her talents can stay low. Her Burst is the part that matters, so give her Energy first.

Constellations

Cistellula Mira · Low priority. C0 does the job. C1 gives the team 80 EM, C4 cuts her Energy needs, and C6 raises reaction damage for the team.

  1. C1

    The Theory of Ash—Field Equilibrium

    After Aino uses her Elemental Skill Musecatcher or her Elemental Burst Precision Hydronic Cooler, her Elemental Mastery will be increased by 80. The Elemental Mastery of other nearby active party members will be increased by 80 for 15s.

    The Elemental Mastery-increasing effects of this Constellation do not stack.

  2. C2

    The Principle of Transference in Gear Differentials

    If Aino is off-field while the Focused Hydronic Cooling Zone of her Elemental Burst Precision Hydronic Cooler is active, when your active party member hits a nearby opponent with an attack, the Cool Your Jets Ducky will fire an additional water ball at that opponent, dealing AoE Hydro DMG equal to 25% of Aino's ATK and 100% of her Elemental Mastery. This DMG is considered Elemental Burst DMG. This effect can be triggered once every 5s.

  3. C3 · +3 Precision Hydronic Cooler

    Cake and the Art of Mechanism Repair

    Increases the Level of Precision Hydronic Cooler by 3.
    Maximum upgrade level is 15.

  4. C4

    Butter and Cats and the Law of Energy Supply

    When the Elemental Skill Musecatcher hits an opponent, it will restore 10 Elemental Energy for Aino. Energy can be restored to her in this manner once every 10s.

  5. C5 · +3 Musecatcher

    Perpetual Turbine of Metal and Light

    Increases the Level of Musecatcher by 3.
    Maximum upgrade level is 15.

  6. C6

    The Burden of Creative Genius

    For the next 15s after using the Elemental Burst Precision Hydronic Cooler, DMG from nearby active characters' Electro-Charged, Bloom, Lunar-Charged, Lunar-Bloom, and Lunar-Crystallize reactions is increased by 15%.
    Moonsign: Ascendant Gleam: DMG from the aforementioned reactions will be further increased by 20%.
