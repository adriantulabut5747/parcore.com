    Ask Klee.

    KleeKlee has met every single one of them! Tell Klee what else you want to know.

    What do New and On banner mean?

    New = released in the last 42 days, about one version. On banner = on a character Event Wish right now. Hover or tap a card to see when their last banner ended.

    Why is the Traveler only one card?

    Aether and Lumine play exactly the same, so the twins share one card. It's listed under every element the Traveler has unlocked.

    Why are some characters under Other?

    Aloy, Skirk and Nicole aren't tied to any nation of Teyvat, so By nation puts them in their own Other band.

    When do new characters show up?

    The roster refreshes every three hours from Project Amber, so a new character joins within hours of their release. Your filters stay in the address, so a filtered list can be shared.
