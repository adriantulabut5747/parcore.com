Her story

Prima Donna of the Korolevskiy Troupe and a Water Imp who has parted from her kin.

A long, long time ago in Snezhnaya, Water Imps dwelled all over the city. Some sang glorious melodies on the stage, while others cheerfully hummed lighthearted tunes through the streets and alleyways. Then there were the others who preferred to slip into the water, where they would lure curious passersby to the water's edge with their haunting, dreamlike songs, only to then suddenly leap up and splash the unsuspecting onlookers full in the face before vanishing, giggling, back beneath the surface with a flick of their tails, leaving their victims floundering between anger and laughter...

Read more

But, as time passed, the stages and alleyways of Snezhnaya quietly lost their singers, and the melodies beneath the waters, carried by unseen currents, began flowing soundlessly toward a distant silence. People began to whisper uneasily among themselves, wondering if those haunting songs from the deep might drift away from them forever...

Then, one morning, people learned that Vodyanitsa was still performing on the stage of Korolevskiy Theater, as though the days of old had never faded and the joy of bygone times had remained just as it always had been.

Hardly anyone knew why she stayed — nor did anyone dare ask her to her face, unless they were prepared to never secure another interview with Miss Vodyanitsa ever again.

Maybe, she had stayed on in order not to disappoint the audiences who so adored her singing? Or maybe she was just unwilling to exit that dazzling stage? Or perhaps... there were other reasons significant enough to make her swim against the current, even if it meant bidding farewell to her own kind...

But for Vodyanitsa's audience, delving into the answer to that question is utterly meaningless. The only thing that matters is that they can continue to listen to the captivating voice of the Water Imp.

Of course, if you want to be swept away by her song...

"Standing tickets? Uh, sorry, but we don't sell those here at the theater at the moment... And no, we don't allow our patrons to bring their own chairs either."

"Crack the doors open a little? The draft would leave everyone inside freezing..."

"My apologies, but the theater isn't hiring any day cleaners at the moment!"

...First, you need to get a ticket.

Vodyanitsa uses her Skill for her Song of Ages Past, which summons the Horn of Spring's Call to attack and lower enemies' Hydro and Cryo RES. It also heals the active character at intervals and makes them harder to interrupt. Her Skill also gives stacks that add damage to teammates' hits. So she's a natural fit for Hydro and Cryo teams and Stellar Swirl. Her Burst is mostly filler, and all of her main stats are HP.

Elemental Skill

Rechitativ: Sonorous Dawn

Cooldown16sC3+3 levelsLevel to10

Reveals her true Water Imp form, dealing AoE Hydro DMG and gaining the Song of Ages Past effect: While this effect is active, Vodyanitsa summons the ancient Horn of Spring's Call, periodically moving closer to an opponent in a swift movement to attack them, dealing Hydro DMG.
The above two types of attacks will also decrease the Hydro RES and Cryo RES of opponents hit. Additionally, while the Song of Ages Past effect is active, your active character gains increased resistance to interruption and periodically regenerates HP based on Vodyanitsa's Max HP.

Why level 10: Her heal, RES drop and damage. Your first Crown goes here.

1st Ascension Passive

The Last Djeguako Songstress

When the Elemental Skill Rechitativ: Sonorous Dawn is used, any Stellar Vortex on the field will turn into a Wandering Vortex instead. While the Song of Ages Past effect is active, nearby party members' Stellar Swirl reactions will generate Wandering Vortex instead.
Generating or detonating a Wandering Vortex will also reduce nearby opponents' Anemo RES by 35% for 6s.
Additionally, nearby party members who enter the Radiance: Stellar Swirl state while Song of Ages Past is in effect will have the Radiance: Stellar Swirl state extended by 4s.

4th Ascension Passive

Dirge of the Fandyr

When the Elemental Skill Rechitativ: Sonorous Dawn is used, Vodyanitsa will also gain 25 stacks of Lead Vocal as well as 10 stacks of Chorus.
The two effects above last 30s each, and the stack count is refreshed when the Elemental Skill is used.

Passive

Echoes Over the Frozen Lake

Holding the Elemental Skill outside of combat will cause Vodyanitsa to embody her true Water Imp form and swim for up to 10s at the cost of Stamina. Naturally, as a Water Imp, she can also move across the water's surface. Vodyanitsa will exit her Water Imp form when the Elemental Skill is used again, when the duration ends, or when all Stamina is depleted.
Additionally, while Vodyanitsa can swim faster, her Stamina consumption rate is also increased.

Passive

Heartless Siren Song

As the Prima Donna of the Korolevskiy Troupe, Vodyanitsa sings in person in place of playing an instrument.

Constellations

Piscicula Aurea · Medium priority. C0 works. C1 gives team ATK from her Max HP and beats R1 of her signature, C2 adds CRIT DMG, and C6 is the capstone.

  1. C1

    Waters in Full Splendor

    Each time Vodyanitsa triggers the healing effect of Song of Ages Past, it also increases the ATK of all nearby party members by 0.8% of her Max HP for 5s.

  2. C2

    Echoes That Pierce the Snow

    When the Horn of Spring's Call from the Elemental Skill Rechitativ: Sonorous Dawn attacks the opponent, Vodyanitsa gains the "Two Voices in Black and White" effect which increases the CRIT DMG from Hydro DMG and Cryo DMG dealt by your own currently active party member by 50% for 5s.
    If there is a Wandering Vortex present on the field or in the 5s after a Wandering Vortex explodes, the effect changes to: increases the CRIT DMG from Stellar Swirl reaction DMG caused by your own currently active party member by 60% for 5s.
    Additionally, the Elemental Skill Rechitativ: Sonorous Dawn is enhanced as follows: the duration of the Song of Ages Past effect is extended by 9s.

  3. C3 · +3 Rechitativ: Sonorous Dawn

    Song Lingering on a Spring Morning

    Increases the Level of the Elemental Skill Rechitativ: Sonorous Dawn by 3.
    Maximum upgrade level is 15.

  4. C4

    Melancholic Voice Upon the Gentle Waters

    When Vodyanitsa triggers the healing effect of Song of Ages Past, an additional effect will be triggered based on the healed character's HP.
    · If the character's HP is below 40%: The healing is increased by 50%; and
    · If the character's HP is 40% or above: Vodyanitsa's Max HP is increased by 20% for 6s. Max 3 stacks, and each stack's duration is counted independently.

  5. C5 · +3 Koda: Sink With Thee

    Together, Down Into the Deep

    Increases the Level of the Elemental Burst Koda: Sink With Thee by 3.
    Maximum upgrade level is 15.

  6. C6

    Neverending Song of Revelry

    The Constellation Echoes That Pierce the Snow is enhanced: "Two Voices in Black and White" now applies to all nearby party members.
    Additionally, while the Song of Ages Past effect is active, Stellar Swirl reaction DMG dealt by nearby party members is elevated by 25% while Hydro DMG and Cryo DMG dealt are increased by 60%.
