Her story

The Moon Maiden born in Nod-Krai, and also the welkin moon returning home.

Day or night?

Read more

Why, night, of course. The world of daylight was filled with too many words beyond her understanding. "Kuutar," "The Damselette," "Goddess"... She knew these words to be like mirrors, each reflecting a version of her in this unfamiliar world. But those were versions others wished to see. She did not recognize herself in those reflections, just as she did not recognize the moon hanging in the sky. At least at night, she could clearly feel the pull coming from beyond the heavens. It made no sound, yet she understood it all the same — the Frost Moon was calling her home.

...

Spring, summer, autumn, or winter?

Winter, without question. After the restlessness of spring, the clamor of summer, and the bounty of autumn, winter's snow quietly laid a finger across the world's lips, as if to say, "Hush. After all that bustle, isn't it time for some quiet?"

And so the world would obediently fall silent. Little animals would retreat to their homes as well. Only in winter would people truly realize how important a warm "home" could be.

As for Columbina? She loved to find a thick, soft pile of snow, before holding her breath and diving straight in. For a goddess who wasn't particularly sensitive to cold, being wrapped head to toe in gentle white snow felt simply wonderful. As to whether her colleagues understood this behavior... Well, she concerned herself little with that.

...

Sunny? Rainy? Or perhaps, cloudy?

A rainy morning. When Columbina woke to the soft patter of rain beyond the Silvermoon Hall, a sense of calm would settle over her — the feeling of being sheltered beneath its vaulted roof.

"Shelter." In the rhythm of the rain, the word resonated with renewed strength, echoing the most essential meaning that "home" could hold. Simply existing along the extension of that meaning was enough to fill Columbina with warmth.

Still... nightfall was better when under clear skies.

...

Were her answers to these questions truly accurate? Might they change someday? Columbina turned these thoughts over in her mind.

Probably not. But even if they did, these answers were the truest version of herself in this moment — the truest version of her on this night.

Forests, lakes, and open seas... Which did she love most?

She loved them all, of course.

Columbina's Skill starts a Gravity Ripple that follows the active character, deals Hydro DMG and fills up when the team triggers Lunar reactions. She turns Electro-Charged, Bloom and Hydro Crystallize into their Lunar versions for the whole team and raises the Moonsign by one level, so she fits almost any Lunar team. Her Burst adds a Lunar Domain with more reaction DMG. She needs a lot of Energy to use it every rotation.

1st Ascension Passive

Lunacy's Lure

When triggering Gravity Interference, Columbina gains the Lunacy effect, increasing her CRIT Rate by 5% for 10s. This effect can be stacked up to 3 times.

4th Ascension Passive

Law of the New Moon

When characters within the Lunar Domain trigger Lunar reactions, they will gain the following effects respectively:
· Lunar-Charged: When the Thundercloud performs lightning strike(s) against a valid target, it has a 33% chance to perform an additional lightning strike.
· Lunar-Bloom: When triggering Lunar-Bloom reactions, the party gains a special Moonridge Dew. Up to 3 Moonridge Dews can be gained every 18s.
· Lunar-Crystallize: When Moondrift Harmony is triggered, every Moondrift has a 33% chance to inflict an extra attack.

Passive

Moonsign Benediction: Moonlight, Lent Unto You

When a party member triggers an Electro-Charged, Bloom, or Hydro-Crystallize reaction, it will be converted into a Lunar-Charged, Lunar-Bloom, or Lunar-Crystallize reaction respectively. Lunar Reaction DMG inflicted by party members gain a Base DMG increase based on Columbina's Max HP. For every 1,000 points of Max HP, the Base DMG of Lunar reactions is increased by 0.2%, up to a maximum of 7%.

Additionally, when Columbina is in the party, the party's Moonsign will increase by 1 level.

Passive

Lunar Vigil

If one of your party members is incapacitated while in the Nod-Krai or Frost Moon regions, Columbina will revive this character. The amount of HP the character will have upon revival is based on Columbina's Friendship Level. This effect can occur once every 100s. This effect does not trigger in Domains, Trounce Domains, or the Spiral Abyss.

    Radiance Over Blossoms and Peaks

    Unleashing her Elemental Skill Eternal Tides will immediately trigger an effect equivalent to the effect of Gravity Interference. This effect can be triggered once every 15s.
    Moonsign: Ascendant Gleam
    When Gravity Interference is triggered, if the Lunar reaction type that has accumulated the most Gravity for Columbina is:
    · Lunar-Charged: Your current active character will recover 6 Energy.
    · Lunar-Bloom: Your active character's interruption resistance will be increased for 8s.
    · Lunar-Crystallize: Summons a Rainsea Shield. The Rainsea Shield's DMG absorption is based on 12% of Columbina's Max HP, and it will absorb Hydro DMG with 250% effectiveness for 8s.

    All nearby party members' Lunar Reaction DMG is elevated by 1.5%.

    Not in Lone Splendor

    Rate of accumulating Gravity increases by 34%.

    When Lunar Brilliance is active, if, during the current trigger of Gravity Interference, the Lunar reaction type that has accumulated the most Gravity for Columbina is:
    · Lunar-Charged: ATK of your current active character increases. Increase in ATK is equal to 1% of Columbina's Max HP.
    · Lunar-Bloom: Elemental Mastery of your current active character increases. Increase in Elemental Mastery is equal to 0.35% of Columbina's Max HP.
    · Lunar-Crystallize: DEF of your current active character increases. Increase in DEF is equal to 1% of Columbina's Max HP.

    All nearby party members' Lunar Reaction DMG is elevated by 7%.

    All nearby party members' Lunar Reaction DMG is elevated by 7%.
