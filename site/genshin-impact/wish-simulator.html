How the odds work.

The game's published rates, and the community's soft-pity model that the simulator rolls with.

Is this the game's real luck?

The base rates, hard pity, 50/50, 75/25 and Epitomized Path are HoYoverse's published rules. Soft pity isn't published: the rising chance from wish 74 (63 on the weapon wish) is the community's model, measured from millions of logged wishes. The game's own random numbers stay secret, so no simulator can predict your next pull.

How does Capturing Radiance work here?

HoYoverse says it lifts the 50/50 to a 55% consolidated chance, and that losing three 50/50s in a row guarantees the next one. The exact counter isn't published, so the simulator uses the community's model: each loss adds one, each win takes one off, and at three the next 50/50 is won by Radiance. That lands at about 54.5% over many wishes.

Do pity and the guarantee carry over?

Yes, like in the game. Both character wishes share one pity and one guarantee, and they carry to the next version's banners. The weapon wish and the Standard wish each keep their own. Epitomized Path's Fate Point resets when the weapon wish ends.

What do duplicates turn into?

A repeat 4★ character unlocks a Constellation and gives 2 Masterless Starglitter (5 once they're at C6); a repeat 5★ gives 10 (25 past C6). Every 4★ weapon gives 2 Starglitter, every 5★ weapon 10, every 3★ weapon 15 Masterless Stardust.

Why do the 3★ reveals come first?

A ten-wish shows its pulls from the lowest rarity up, so the best one comes last. The summary afterwards lists them best first, characters before weapons, like the game's result screen.
