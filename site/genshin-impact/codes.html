    How to redeem.

    KleeKlee redeems codes in four taps. Follow the cursor and do what Klee does!

    1. Press Esc on PC, or tap Paimon in the top-left corner on mobile. Codes unlock at Adventure Rank 10.

    2. Tap the gear, then the Account tab on the left.

    3. Next to Redeem Code, tap Redeem Now. Paste the code (Copy above puts it on your clipboard) and tap Exchange.

    4. The rewards arrive in your in-game Mail, the envelope in the Paimon Menu. Claim them before the letter expires.

    Why does a code say it's invalid?

    Codes only work from Adventure Rank 10, once per account, and only until they expire. Check the spelling (Copy above avoids typos). If it's listed under Expired codes, it's gone for good.

    Where are my rewards?

    In your in-game Mail, the envelope in the Paimon Menu, whether you redeemed in the game or on the gift page. Claim them before the letter expires.

    Can I redeem without opening the game?

    Yes. Redeem opens HoYoverse's official gift page with the code filled in. Log in, pick your server and character, and the rewards are mailed to you in the game.

    When do new codes come out?

    Most arrive during the Special Program livestream before each new version, three at a time, and those expire quickly. Others come with events and collaborations. This page checks for new codes every three hours.
