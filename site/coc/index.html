    Frequently asked questions

    How do I copy a base into my game?

    Pick your Town Hall under Featured Townhalls, then tap Copy Layout under any base. Clash of Clans opens with the layout loaded, so just save it to one of your layout slots.

    Why won’t the Copy Layout link open the game?

    Apps like Discord, Facebook and Messenger open links in their own built-in browser, which can’t launch the game. Tap the ⋮ menu, choose Open in Chrome (or your usual browser), then tap Copy Layout again.

    Can I use a base made for a different Town Hall?

    Only one level away. Base links work one Town Hall level up or down, and anything you haven’t unlocked yet is simply left out. Any further than that and the link won’t copy.

    Are these bases good for war, CWL or trophy pushing?

    Yes. We choose quality over quantity. We only post bases that have proven themselves, so every layout here is built to hold up in war, CWL and on the ladder.

    How do I join Ascendere?

    Join our Global Chat and let us know you’re interested. We’ll reply and set you up with a quick tryout. We pick a base, you attack it, and a clean three-star earns your spot. You’ll need Town Hall 11 or higher.
