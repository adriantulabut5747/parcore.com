        Ask the Villager

        VillagerI can tell you a thing or two, Chief! Don’t see your question? Send it with the form.

        How do I copy a base into my game?

        Open your Town Hall under Featured Town Halls and tap Copy Layout under any base. Clash of Clans opens with the layout loaded: save it to one of your layout slots and it’s yours.

        Why won’t Copy Layout open the game?

        Discord, Facebook and Messenger open links in their own built-in browser, which can’t launch the game. Tap the ⋮ menu, choose Open in Chrome (or your usual browser), then tap Copy Layout again.

        Can I use a base made for a different Town Hall?

        Only one level away. Base links work one Town Hall up or down, and anything you haven’t unlocked yet is left out. Any further than that and the link won’t copy.

        Are these bases good for war, CWL and trophy pushing?

        Yes. Quality over quantity: a base only goes up after it has proven itself, so every layout here is built to hold in war, CWL and on the ladder.

        Where does the live data come from?

        Straight from Supercell’s official Clash of Clans API. The war card refreshes every two minutes. The top players and the equipment picks are saved once a week, two hours before the Monday league reset, so you see the week’s final standings.

        Do I need an account to use the tracker?

        No. It’s free and there’s no sign-in. Type a clan’s name or any #tag in the tracker above and press Scout.

        Why can’t I see my clan’s war?

        Supercell only shares a war when the clan’s war log is public. A leader or co-leader can switch on Public War Log in the clan’s settings, and the war shows up here on the next refresh.

        How is “most picked” equipment counted?

        We check every hero of the 200 highest-ranked players on the global leaderboard and count what each one has equipped. 91% means 91 of every 100 of those players who own that hero had it on.

        How do I join Ascendere?

        Join our Global Chat and tell us you’re interested. We’ll set you up with a quick tryout: we pick a base, you attack it, and a clean three-star earns your spot. You’ll need Town Hall 11 or higher.
