Frequently asked questions

How do I copy an army into my game?

Tap Copy Army under any army. Clash of Clans opens with the troops, spells, heroes and Clan Castle already filled in, so just save it to one of your army slots.

Why won’t the Copy Army link open the game?

Apps like Discord, Facebook and Messenger open links in their own built-in browser, which can’t launch the game. Tap the ⋮ menu, choose Open in Chrome (or your usual browser), then tap Copy Army again.

What do the Legend I, II and III badges mean?

They mark our three strongest armies right now: Legend I is our top pick, then Legend II and Legend III. It’s our ranking, not the league the army was used in.

Are these armies good for war, CWL and ranked?

Yes. Every army here is one we’ve used in war, CWL and ranked battles. Practise a new one in a friendly challenge first, because the same army can fail if the spells and heroes go in at the wrong time.

How do I join Ascendere?

Join our Global Chat and let us know you’re interested. We’ll reply and set you up with a quick tryout. We pick a base, you attack it, and a clean three-star earns your spot. You’ll need Town Hall 11 or higher.
