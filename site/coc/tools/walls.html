Frequently asked questions

Can I use elixir for walls?

Yes, from level 5 up. The cost is the same in gold or elixir, so the totals here work for either.

How many walls can I have?

325 from Town Hall 14 up, 300 at Town Hall 11 to 13, and fewer below that.

What about Wall Rings?

Wall Rings replace the gold or elixir for one wall’s upgrade. You need one ring per million of the cost, rounded up: one up to level 12, then 2 for levels 13 and 14, up to 10 for level 19. The Gold Pass doesn’t lower the number of rings.

Does the Gold Pass make walls cheaper?

Yes. The Builder Boost from the season challenges lowers wall costs by 10%, 15% or 20%, depending on how far into the season you are. Pick your boost above and the totals update.

How do Hammer Jam and the Gold Pass add up?

They multiply, so 50% and 20% make 60% off, not 70%. Hammer Jam halves the price first, then the Gold Pass takes 20% off what’s left: a 10M wall drops to 5M, then to 4M. Not every Hammer Jam has covered walls, so check that the wall price in game shows the discount before you spend.
