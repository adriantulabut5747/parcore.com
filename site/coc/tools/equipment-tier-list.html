Frequently asked questions

How do I share my tier list?

Press Copy link. The link holds your whole ranking and your tier names, so whoever opens it sees the same list. To load a link someone sent you, press Import list and paste it.

Can I make a list for one hero only?

Yes. Pick the hero above the board. Only that hero’s equipment shows, and the link and the image keep that choice.

Can I add more tiers or rename them?

Yes. + Add tier adds a row under the last one, up to 10, and the × on a row removes it. Its pieces go back to Unranked. Tap a tier’s name to change it to any text, up to 20 letters.

Is my list saved?

It’s saved in the page’s address. Bookmark it or copy the link before you leave, or it’s gone.
