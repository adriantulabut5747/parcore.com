Tips

  • Mending first. An item with Mending repairs itself from XP orbs, 2 durability per point. Hold it while you farm XP and you never need an anvil.
  • Use materials, not a second item. On an anvil, one unit of the material fixes a quarter of the item for 1 level, cheaper than giving up a second item.
  • Watch the prior work. Every anvil job doubles the next one's base cost (+1, +3, +7, +15, +31). After about six jobs the item hits Too Expensive.
  • Rename on a job you're already doing. Renaming costs 1 level and doesn't add an anvil use if you do it in the same job.
  • Grindstones are free but wipe enchantments. Fine for plain tools; never for gear with Mending or Protection.
  • Wood, stone and gold are cheap to remake. Crafting a new one is often quicker than repairing it.

    • Durability values are Java Edition 26.3. The grey part of each bar is what the item had before.
    • A job of 40 levels or more shows Too Expensive! in Survival.
