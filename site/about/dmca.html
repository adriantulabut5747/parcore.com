DMCA and copyright

You own something that appears on Parchrome without permission? Here is what to send and what happens next.

Last updated November 2, 2026Notices: wildparchrome@gmail.com

01What we can act on

Parchrome publishes its own guides, layouts and tools, and links to sites run by other people. We can remove or change anything on our own pages, including a link or listing. We can't remove material hosted on another site, so send that request to the site that hosts it as well.

02How to send a notice

Email wildparchrome@gmail.com with DMCA notice in the subject line. A valid notice includes:

  1. Your physical or electronic signature (typing your full name counts);
  2. The copyrighted work you believe is being infringed;
  3. The exact Parchrome page address and, if it is a link, which link;
  4. Your name, postal address, phone number and email address;
  5. A statement that you believe in good faith the use is not authorized by the owner, its agent or the law;
  6. A statement that the information in the notice is accurate and, under penalty of perjury, that you are the owner or authorized to act for the owner.

A notice missing these details may be delayed while we ask for them. Knowingly false claims can make the sender liable for damages.

03What happens next

We review each complete notice and remove or disable the reported material or link when the claim is valid. We may contact you if something is unclear.

04Counter-notice

If something you sent us (for example a suggested website) was removed and you believe that was a mistake, email us with: your signature, the removed material and where it appeared, a statement under penalty of perjury that you believe it was removed by mistake, and your name, address and phone number with your consent to the jurisdiction of your local federal court (or, outside the US, any court where we may be found).

05Repeat infringement

When a listed site or resource is shown to be mainly used for infringement, we remove it from Parchrome entirely instead of dealing with it one link at a time.

06Game companies

If you represent a publisher whose game appears on Parchrome and want something changed or taken down, email wildparchrome@gmail.com. We are a fan site and will work with you.
