Privacy Policy

Exactly what Parchrome stores, what gets sent when you look up a player, and how to have your data deleted. No account, no ads, nothing sold.

Last updated November 2, 202611 sections

01The short version

  • No accountWe never ask you to sign up, so we hold no profiles or passwords.
  • Settings stay with youYour picks, recent searches and likes are saved in your own browser.
  • Lookups go to game dataA tag, Riot ID or UID you search is sent to the service that has that player's data.
  • Forms are storedFeedback and suggestions are kept so we can read them. We never sell anything.

02What we collect and why

This is everything Parchrome itself stores about visitors:

WhatWhenStored inWhy
Feedback: type, message, star rating, your email or in-game name, the page address and the timeYou send the feedback formGoogle Firebase (Firestore)To read it, reply and fix things
Valorant searches: the Riot name, tag and region searched, your browser's user-agent string and the timeYou look up a Valorant playerGoogle Firebase (Firestore)To see how the tracker is used
Like counts on Clash of Clans layouts and armies (a number, not who liked)You tap a likeGoogle Firebase (Firestore)To show how many people liked it
Visit statistics: pages viewed, device and browser type, rough locationYou open the Clash of Clans hub (/coc/)Google AnalyticsTo count visits

We don't sell, rent or trade any of it, and we don't use it for advertising.

03Player lookups

When you search for a player, the identifier you type has to be sent to the service that holds that player's data. These identifiers are public in the games themselves.

LookupWhat is sentWho receives it
Clash of Clans player or clanThe player or clan tagOur server (Cloudflare), then Supercell's Clash of Clans API through RoyaleAPI's proxy; player history from ClashKing
Valorant playerRiot name, tag and regionOur server (Cloudflare), then HenrikDev API
Genshin Impact playerThe UIDOur server (Netlify), then Enka.Network
Minecraft player or skinThe usernamePlayerDB, MC-Heads and Crafatar
Minecraft server statusThe server addressmcsrvstat.us

Like any website, these services also see the internet (IP) address of whoever contacts them: our servers for the first three, your browser for the Minecraft ones.

04Saved in your browser

Some things are saved only in your browser's local storage, never sent to us:

  • Recent player searches on the trackers;
  • Which layouts and armies you liked, so the heart stays filled;
  • Your picks on tools (for example your Genshin server, farming list or wish tab, a Minecraft seed view, Clash of Clans wall settings);
  • The times you sent feedback (no message content), to limit spam to 5 sends every 10 minutes.

Clearing your browser's site data removes all of it. It doesn't sync between devices.

05Cookies

Parchrome doesn't set cookies of its own. Google Analytics, which runs only on the Clash of Clans hub page, sets its own cookies to count visits. No page uses advertising or cross-site tracking cookies.

06Services that see your visit

Loading a page also loads files from these providers, who receive your IP address and browser details as part of normal web traffic:

  • Netlify hosts the site;
  • Cloudflare runs our player lookup servers;
  • Google serves our fonts (Google Fonts) and, when a form or like button is used, Firebase;
  • jsDelivr serves a code library on one article page;
  • Game art is loaded from official asset servers (for example Supercell's) and from the services in the lookup table above.

Each one handles that data under its own privacy policy.

07How long we keep it

Feedback, suggestions and the Valorant search log are kept until they are no longer useful to us, or sooner if you ask us to delete them. Analytics data follows Google Analytics' retention settings.

08Your choices and rights

You can ask for a copy of what we hold about you, or ask us to correct or delete it. Email wildparchrome@gmail.com and include the email address or in-game name you used so we can find it. Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR or CCPA), you may have further rights, and you can complain to your local data protection authority.

To stop Google Analytics, use a tracker blocker or Google's Analytics opt-out add-on.

09Children

Parchrome isn't aimed at children under 13 and we don't knowingly collect their information. If you believe a child has sent us something through a form, email wildparchrome@gmail.com and we'll delete it.

10Changes to this policy

When the site starts storing something new, this page is updated first. The date at the top shows the latest version.

11Contact

Questions about your data or this policy? Email wildparchrome@gmail.com.
