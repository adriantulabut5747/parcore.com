Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 2026

This page explains what data Parchrome collects, why, and how it's used. We try to keep this to a minimum.

1. Information stored in your browser (localStorage)

Parchrome saves a few things directly in your browser's local storage — never sent to us or any server. This includes:

  • Bookmarked tools and websites you've saved (e.g. from the Web Resources directory);
  • Display preferences, like compact-view toggles;
  • A short, local log of feedback-form submission times, used only to prevent spam (it does not leave your browser and contains no message content).

This data stays on your device. Clearing your browser's site data or cache will remove it, and it won't sync across devices or browsers.

2. Feedback you submit

If you use the feedback form (bug reports, suggestions, or ratings), we store what you submit — your message, an optional email or in-game name (IGN), the feedback type/rating, the page you submitted from, and a timestamp — in our database (Firebase/Firestore) so we can read and act on it. We use this only to improve the site and don't sell or share it with third parties. Don't include sensitive personal information in a feedback submission.

3. Analytics

Some pages use Google Analytics to understand overall traffic and usage patterns (e.g. which pages are popular). This is aggregated and anonymized usage data — we don't use it to identify individual visitors. You can opt out of Google Analytics tracking using a browser extension like Google's own Analytics Opt-out add-on, or by using tracker-blocking browser settings/extensions.

4. Third-party sites

Parchrome links to third-party tools, sites, and a Discord server we don't control. Once you leave Parchrome, that site's own privacy policy applies — we're not responsible for how they handle your data.

5. Cookies

We don't use cookies for advertising or cross-site tracking. Any cookies present come from the analytics or third-party services described above.

6. Children's privacy

Parchrome isn't directed at children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

7. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy as the site changes. Continued use of Parchrome after updates means you accept the revised policy.

8. Contact

Questions about your data or this policy? Reach out through our Discord.
