About

One site for the games you play

Parchrome is a free, independent fan site. Clash of Clans base layouts and army comps, Minecraft tools, Valorant and Genshin Impact player stats, CODM loadouts and game guides, all in one place and all without an account.

Free, no sign-upNo adsFan-made

01What you will find here

Every game we cover, with what is on it right now. Tap one to jump in.

02How we build it

  • Real data onlyStats come from the games' own data services. If a number isn't known, we leave it blank instead of guessing.
  • No accountEvery page and tool works without signing up or logging in.
  • No adsNo banners, no pop-ups and no ad trackers on any page.
  • Fixed when wrongSpot a mistake? Send it through the feedback form and it gets corrected.

03Where the numbers come from

Live numbers are pulled from these services when you open a page or look up a player. We don't own them, and when one is down, the page tells you instead of showing old or made-up data.

GameWhatSource
Clash of ClansPlayers, clans, leaderboardsSupercell's official Clash of Clans API, through our own server; player history from ClashKing
Clash RoyaleDecks, cards, win ratesSupercell's official Clash Royale API
ValorantPlayer stats and matchesHenrikDev API, through our own server; agent and map art from valorant-api.com; esports events and news from VLR.gg
Genshin ImpactPlayer showcasesEnka.Network, through our own server; item data from Project Amber
MinecraftSkins, servers, modsMojang, PlayerDB, MC-Heads, Crafatar, mcsrvstat.us and Modrinth

04Who is behind it

Parchrome is an independent project. It is not run, paid for or endorsed by Supercell, Riot Games, HoYoverse, Activision, Mojang, Microsoft, Bandai Namco or any other game company. All game names, art and trademarks belong to their owners. The full notices are at the bottom of every page and in our Terms.

Layouts, tier lists and articles are written by us. Game art is used to show which game, character or item a page is about.

05Help us out

The best ways to help are free: tell a clanmate, send a fix when something is wrong, or ask for the guide you wish existed.
