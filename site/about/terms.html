The site is free and needs no account. You may use it for your own, non-commercial purposes: reading guides, copying base layouts into your game, using the tools and looking up players. You must be old enough to use the games' own services where you live.

Only look up public game identifiers (tags, Riot IDs, UIDs, usernames); don't use the lookups to harass anyone.
